If you are looking for the best corn maze in Michigan, you want a farm that combines a challenging maze with family-friendly activities and good value. After visiting and researching dozens of farms across the state, the top picks for 2026 are DeBuck's Corn Maze in Belleville, The Corn Maze at Gallup Park in Ann Arbor (though smaller, it is free), and Wiard's Orchard & Country Fair in Ypsilanti. Each offers a unique experience, and the right choice depends on your location and what you want beyond the maze.

## What Makes a Corn Maze Great

A great corn maze is more than just a field with paths. Look for these features:

- **Design complexity**: The best mazes have multiple checkpoints and bridges for a bird's-eye view. DeBuck's, for example, has a 10-acre maze with a theme that changes yearly.
- **Extra activities**: Most top farms offer hayrides, pumpkin patches, and farm animals. This makes the trip worth the drive, especially with kids.
- **Accessibility**: Wide paths and clear signage matter, especially for strollers or those with mobility issues.
- **Clean facilities**: Porta-potties are standard, but the best farms have real restrooms and hand-washing stations.
- **Value

## Top Corn Mazes in Michigan for 2026

Here are the standout farms, listed by region. All are open in September and October, with some starting in late August.

### Southeast Michigan

**DeBuck's Corn Maze** (Belleville)
- **Maze size**: 10 acres, one of the largest in the state.
- **Extras**: 40+ attractions including a giant slide, corn cannon, and pig races. They also have a 5-acre sunflower field in late summer.
- **Tickets**: $16.95 for adults, $13.95 for kids (ages 3-12) in 2026. Discounts for groups of 20+.
- **Hours**: Open daily 10 AM to 10 PM in October, but check their website for September hours.
- **Why it wins**: The maze is genuinely challenging, and the farm is well-organized. Expect crowds on weekends, so go on a weekday if you can.

**Wiard's Orchard & Country Fair** (Ypsilanti)
- **Maze size**: 5 acres, with a separate kid's maze.
- **Extras**: Apple picking, cider donuts, and a petting zoo. The country fair includes a corn pit and a jumping pillow.
- **Tickets**: $14 for adults, $12 for kids (ages 3-12) in 2026. Includes all activities except apple picking.
- **Hours**: Open daily 9 AM to 7 PM, with special evening hours for the maze until 10 PM on Fridays and Saturdays.
- **Why it wins**: It is a full day out with excellent food options. The maze is less intense than DeBuck's, making it better for families with young children.

### West Michigan

**Robinette's Apple Haus & Winery** (Grand Rapids)
- **Maze size**: 3 acres, but the farm has 25 acres of apple orchards.
- **Extras**: Hard cider tasting, apple cider slushies, and a bakery. The maze is free with a $5 parking fee, which is a bargain.
- **Tickets**: Free maze, but you pay for parking ($5 per car) and any activities like the hayride ($3 per person).
- **Hours**: Open daily 9 AM to 6 PM, with the maze open until 8 PM in October.
- **Why it wins**: It is a budget-friendly option with a beautiful setting. The maze is smaller, but you can spend hours exploring the orchards and tasting room.

### Northern Michigan

**The Corn Maze at The Maples** (Kingsley)
- **Maze size**: 7 acres, with a 2-mile path.
- **Extras**: Corn cannon, jumping pillow, and a 60-foot slide. They also have a haunted maze on Friday and Saturday nights in October.
- **Tickets**: $15 for adults, $12 for kids (ages 4-12) in 2026. Haunted maze is an extra $5.
- **Hours**: Open daily 10 AM to 9 PM, with the haunted maze starting at 7 PM.
- **Why it wins**: It is the best option for those up north, with a good mix of activities and a spooky twist for older kids.

## How to Choose the Right Maze for You

Consider these factors when picking a farm:

- **Distance**: You do not want to drive two hours if a good maze is closer. Use the list above as a starting point.
- **Group composition**: If you have toddlers, look for a farm with a separate kid's maze and gentle activities. If you have teens, they will prefer a larger maze and thrill rides.
- **Budget**: Add up ticket prices, parking, food, and extras. A family of four can easily spend $80 to $120 at a top farm, so plan ahead.
- **Weather**: Check the forecast. A rainy day means muddy paths, but some farms have covered areas. Call ahead if in doubt.

## Practical Tips for Your Visit

- **Go early**: Arrive at opening time to beat the crowds and have the maze to yourself. The paths are easier to navigate when not packed.
- **Wear comfortable shoes**: You will walk 1 to 3 miles inside the maze. Sneakers or boots are best, not sandals.
- **Bring water**: Even in October, you can get thirsty. Most farms sell water, but it is cheaper to bring your own.
- **Use the app**: Some farms have a maze app that tracks your location. DeBuck's has one, and it saves you from getting lost for hours.
- **Take a photo of the map**: At the entrance, snap a picture of the maze map. It helps if you get turned around.
- **Check for coupons**: Many farms offer discounts on their website or through local tourism boards. A quick search can save you $2 to $5 per ticket.

## FAQ

**When is the best time to visit a corn maze?**

Weekday mornings are ideal. The maze is less crowded, and you can move at your own pace. If you must go on a weekend, arrive right at opening time. Late October can be muddy, so check the forecast.

**Are corn mazes suitable for young children?**

Yes, most farms have a kid's maze that is smaller and easier. DeBuck's and Wiard's both have dedicated areas for kids under 5. Always supervise children, as it is easy to lose sight of them in tall corn.

**Can I bring my dog?**

Most farms do not allow pets, except service animals. The corn can be dusty, and dogs may disturb other visitors. Check the farm's policy before bringing your dog.

**How long does it take to complete a corn maze?**

It depends on the size and your navigation skills. A 5-acre maze can take 45 minutes to 1.5 hours. A 10-acre maze like DeBuck's can take 2 to 3 hours if you hit every checkpoint. Budget at least half a day for the whole farm.

## The Bottom Line

The best corn maze in Michigan is DeBuck's Corn Maze for its size and variety, but Wiard's Orchard is a close second for families, and Robinette's is the budget pick. No matter which you choose, you will get a memorable fall day. Plan ahead, dress for the weather, and bring your sense of adventure. You will not regret it.
