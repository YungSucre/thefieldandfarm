## Garlic: The Set-and-Forget Bulb

Garlic is a fall-planted crop that rewards patience. Plant cloves in October or November (zone dependent), and harvest the following summer. It requires little attention: water occasionally, mulch for winter, and pull weeds. The payoff is a pantry staple that stores for months.

### Planting and Timing

Plant garlic 4 to 6 weeks before the ground freezes, typically mid-October to mid-November in zones 3-7. In warmer zones (8-9), plant in late November or December. Use hardneck varieties for cold climates (zones 3-7) and softneck for mild winters (zones 8-9).

| Zone | Planting Window | Harvest Window |
|------|-----------------|----------------|
| 3-4  | Late Sep to mid-Oct | Late July to Aug |
| 5-6  | Mid-Oct to mid-Nov | Mid-July to Aug |
| 7    | Nov to early Dec | June to July |
| 8-9  | Dec to Jan | May to June |

Break apart bulbs into individual cloves, plant pointy end up, 2 inches deep, 6 inches apart in rows 12 inches apart. Add compost before planting. Mulch with 4-6 inches of straw after the ground freezes to prevent heaving.

### Varieties That Perform

Hardneck: 'Music' (zones 3-7), 'German Extra Hardy' (zones 3-7), 'Chesnok Red' (zones 4-8). Softneck: 'California Early' (zones 8-9), 'Inchelium Red' (zones 5-9). For 2026, expect to pay $10-$15 per pound of seed garlic, which plants about 50 cloves.

### Harvest and Storage

Harvest when lower leaves turn brown but 3-4 green leaves remain. Dig, don't pull, to avoid bruising. Cure in a dry, shaded spot for 2-3 weeks, then trim tops and roots. Store in a cool, dark place (50-60°F) with

## CKD (Leafy Greens): Quick and Continuous

If CKD means chard or kale, these are cool-season crops that thrive in spring and fall. They are fast: you can harvest baby leaves in 30 days, mature leaves in 50-60 days. They tolerate frost, so you can extend the season with row covers.

### Planting and Timing

Direct sow seeds outdoors 2-4 weeks before the last frost date in spring, and again 6-8 weeks before the first frost in fall. For a continuous harvest, sow a new row every 2 weeks. Plant seeds 1/2 inch deep, 1 inch apart, then thin to 6-8 inches apart for full-size plants. Rows 18 inches apart.

| Crop | Spring Planting | Fall Planting | Days to Maturity |
|------|-----------------|---------------|------------------|
| Chard | 2-4 weeks before last frost | 6-8 weeks before first frost | 50-60 |
| Kale | 2-4 weeks before last frost | 6-8 weeks before first frost | 50-65 |

### Varieties That Perform

Chard: 'Bright Lights' (colorful stems), 'Fordhook Giant' (vigorous). Kale: 'Lacinato' (dinosaur), 'Red Russian' (sweet). Seeds cost $3-$5 per packet, enough for a 10-foot row.

### Harvest and Storage

Pick outer leaves as needed, leaving the growing point intact. You can harvest for months. Store in a plastic bag in the fridge for up to a week. For longer storage, blanch and freeze.

## FAQ

**Can I grow garlic in containers?** Yes, use a pot at least 8 inches deep, plant cloves 4 inches apart, and place in full sun. Water regularly, but ensure drainage.

**How do I know when garlic is ready to harvest?** When the lower leaves turn brown, about 40% of the leaves are brown, dig up a bulb to check. The cloves should fill the skin but not split.

**What is the best way to store leafy greens?** Wash and dry thoroughly, wrap in a damp paper towel, and place in a perforated bag in the crisper drawer. Use within 5-7 days.

**Can I plant garlic in spring?** Yes, but you will get smaller bulbs. Plant as early as the ground thaws, and harvest later. For best results, stick to fall planting.

## The Bottom Line

Garlic and CKD serve different purposes. Garlic is a low-effort, high-yield storage crop that requires patience. CKD gives you quick, continuous harvests for fresh eating. If you have space, grow both: plant garlic in fall, and use the same bed for CKD in spring before garlic emerges, or interplant between garlic rows. For 2026, garlic seed costs $10-$15 per pound, and CKD seeds are under $5. Both are worth the investment. Choose based on your needs: garlic for storage, CKD for immediate salads and sautés.
