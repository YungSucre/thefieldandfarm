In Minecraft, a bee nest is a naturally generated block where bees live, while a beehive is the crafted version that does the same job. Both house up to 3 bees, store honey, and can be harvested with shears or glass bottles. The key difference is control: nests appear in the wild, hives you build and place where you want. If you want to start beekeeping, craft a hive. If you find a nest, you can move it with Silk Touch, but a hive is simpler and safer.

## What is a Bee Nest?

A bee nest spawns naturally on oak or birch trees in flower forests, plains, and sunflower plains biomes. It holds up to 3 bees and produces honey over time. You can harvest honeycomb or honey bottles from it, but if you break it without Silk Touch, you get nothing and the bees become hostile. Nests are great for early honey, but they are where they are. ## What is a Beehive?

A beehive is the crafted version. You make it with 6 planks and 3 honeycombs in a crafting table. The honeycombs come from a nest or another hive. Once built, you place it anywhere, and bees will find it if there are flowers nearby. A hive holds up to 3 bees, just like a nest, and produces honey at the same rate. The advantage is total control: you decide where the hive goes, how many hives you have, and you can stack them for a larger operation.

## How to Get Honey and Honeycomb

To harvest honey, use a glass bottle on the block when it is full (honey level 5). You get a honey bottle, which restores 6 hunger points. To get honeycomb, use shears on the full block. You get 3 honeycomb pieces, which you need for crafting hives and candles. Always place a campfire underneath the block before harvesting. The smoke calms the bees, so they do not attack. Without a campfire, expect stings and a swarm.

## When to Use a Hive vs a Nest

Use a nest if you find one early and want quick honey without crafting. But nests are often far from your base, and moving them requires Silk Touch, which is an enchantment you may not have. Use a hive when you want a dedicated apiary. Craft several hives, place them near flowers, and you have a renewable honey source. For a homestead or farm, hives are the way to go. They are cheap, movable, and you can scale up.

## Step-by-Step: Build Your First Hive

1. Find a bee nest. Look for bees flying around oak or birch trees in flower forests or plains. Use a Silk Touch tool to break the nest and collect it. If you do not have Silk Touch, use shears on the nest to get honeycomb (3 pieces) without breaking it.
2. Craft the hive: Place 3 honeycomb in the middle row of a crafting table, and 6 planks around them (top and bottom rows full). This gives you one beehive.
3. Place the hive near flowers. Bees need flowers within 10 blocks to pollinate and fill the hive. Plant a small flower garden nearby.
4. Use a glass bottle or shears on the hive when it is full. Always put a campfire underneath first.

## Common Mistakes and Fixes

- Breaking a nest without Silk Touch: you lose the nest and anger the bees. Fix: always use Silk Touch or shears.
- Harvesting without a campfire: bees attack. Fix: place a campfire directly under the block, even if it is on the ground below.
- Placing hives too far from flowers: bees will not work. Keep flowers within 10 blocks.
- Not enough bees: each hive needs at least 1 bee to produce honey. If you have no bees, lure them with flowers or use a lead.

## FAQ

**Can I breed bees?** Yes. Feed two bees a flower, and they enter love mode and produce a baby bee. The baby grows in 20 minutes. Use this to populate your hives.

**Do bee nests respawn?** No. Once you break a nest, it is gone unless you pick it up with Silk Touch. New nests do not spawn in the same spot. So protect existing nests or craft hives.

**Can I move a bee nest without Silk Touch?** No. Breaking it without Silk Touch destroys the block and drops nothing. You can use shears to get honeycomb, but the nest is lost.

**How many hives do I need for a steady honey supply?** One hive produces honey every few minutes, but for a constant supply, have at least 3 hives. That way, while one is being harvested, others are refilling.

## The Bottom Line

For most players, a beehive is the better choice. It is craftable, movable, and lets you control your apiary. A bee nest is a bonus if you find one, but do not rely on it. Build hives, keep them near flowers, and always use a campfire when harvesting. That is the practical path to a steady honey supply in Minecraft.
