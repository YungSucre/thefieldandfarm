## What to Expect in Terms of Activities and Amenities

Most agriturismi offer a mix of relaxation and work. You can join the morning chores, like feeding chickens or collecting eggs, then spend the afternoon by the pool. Some farms have organized workshops: olive oil tasting, pasta making, or cheese aging. In 2026, many farms have added agrivoltaic systems and solar panels, so you'll see modern sustainability practices alongside traditional ones.

Amenities vary. A basic room includes breakfast with farm products: eggs, cheese, jams, and fresh bread. Self-catering apartments come with a kitchenette, and you can buy farm produce on site. Expect Wi-Fi in common areas, but not always in the rooms. Pools are common, but not universal. If you need a pool, check before booking.

## How to Choose the Right Agriturismo for Your Interests

Start by listing what you want to learn or experience. If you're a vegetable gardener, pick a farm that grows vegetables and offers garden tours or workshops. If you're into livestock, choose a farm with animals. If you're a beekeeper, look for a farm with hives.

Next, check the location. Umbria is hilly, and some farms are remote. If you want to explore towns like Perugia or Assisi, choose a farm within a 30-minute drive. If you want total seclusion, go deeper into the countryside.

Finally, read recent reviews from other gardeners or homesteaders. Look for comments about the farm's authenticity and whether the hosts are welcoming. A good host makes the difference.

## Practical Tips for Booking and Visiting

Book directly through the farm's website or by email. This often gets you a better rate than booking platforms, and you can ask specific questions. In 2026, many farms have online booking, but some still prefer phone or email.

Ask about the minimum stay. Many agriturismi require a two-night minimum in high season (June to September). If you're visiting in spring or fall, you may find shorter stays and lower prices.

Plan for the season. Spring (April to June) is ideal for seeing wildflowers and baby animals. Fall (September to October) is harvest time for grapes and olives, so you can join the harvest. Summer is hot and crowded, but pools are welcome. Winter is quiet, and some farms close.

Bring appropriate clothing: sturdy shoes for farm walks, a hat and sunscreen for summer, and layers for cool evenings. If you plan to help with farm work, bring work gloves.

## FAQ

### Do I need to speak Italian to visit an agriturismo?

No, but it helps. Many hosts speak basic English, especially in tourist areas. Learn a few phrases like "posso aiutare?" (can I help?) and "grazie" (thank you). It goes a long way.

### Can I bring my children to an agriturismo?

Yes, most farms welcome children. They can feed animals, collect eggs, and explore. Some farms have playgrounds or offer babysitting. Check the farm's policy before booking.

### Are agriturismi expensive in 2026?

Prices range from $120 to $350 per night, depending on the season and accommodation type. You get value for money: meals often include farm products, and activities are usually included or low-cost.

### What is the best way to get around Umbria?

Renting a car is the most practical way. Agriturismi are often in the countryside, and public transport is limited. A small car is enough for the narrow roads.
