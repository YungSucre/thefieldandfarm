Farming stars in Dress to Impress (DTI) means earning the in-game currency, Stars, by completing rounds and impressing judges. The fastest way to farm stars is to focus on voting, completing daily tasks, and using themed outfit strategies. Here's how to do it without wasting time.

## Understanding Stars in DTI

Stars are the primary currency in DTI, used to buy clothing, accessories, and custom poses. You earn stars by participating in rounds, receiving votes from other players, and completing specific challenges. The more stars you earn, the more items you can unlock, which helps you create better outfits and win more votes.

## How to Earn Stars Quickly

### 1. Play in Full Servers

Join servers with 10 to 12 players. More players mean more votes per round, which increases your star earnings. Avoid empty servers where you might get few or no votes.

### 2. Vote Strategically

Always vote for other players, even if their outfits are not great. Voting gives you a small star bonus per round. Also, when you vote, you earn a participation reward, which adds up over time.

### 3. Complete Daily Tasks

Check the quests or tasks tab daily. DTI offers daily challenges like "wear a red item" or "use a hat." Completing these gives you bonus stars and sometimes exclusive items.

### 4. Use Themed Outfit Strategies

Each round has a theme. To get more votes, create outfits that match the theme clearly. For example, if the theme is "Cafe," wear an apron, a beret, and hold a coffee cup. Use the search bar in the wardrobe to find themed items quickly.

### 5. Save Your Best Items for Competitive Rounds

Some rounds have higher stakes, like "Free Play" or "Competitive." In these, use your best items and most creative combinations to maximize votes.

## Best Strategies for Winning Votes

### Color Coordination

Use colors that match the theme. For a "Sunset" theme, wear orange, pink, and purple. Avoid clashing colors unless the theme calls for it.

### Layering and Accessories

Layers and accessories make outfits stand out. Add a jacket, scarf, or hat. Use the "layering" feature to combine tops and bottoms for a unique look.

### Follow Trends

Observe what top players wear in the lobby. If many players use a specific item or style, incorporate it into your outfit. Trends change weekly, so stay updated.

### Practice in Free Play

Use Free Play mode to experiment with outfits without pressure. This helps you learn what works and what doesn't, so you can be faster in real rounds.

## Tools and Resources

- **Wardrobe Search**: Use the search bar to find items by name or category. This saves time during rounds.
- **Custom Poses**: Unlock poses that show off your outfit. A good pose can earn extra votes.
- **Style Book**: Create outfit presets for common themes like "Formal," "Casual," or "Fantasy." This lets you apply a full outfit in seconds.

## Realistic Expectations and Numbers

In a typical 10-player round, you can earn 5 to 15 stars per round, depending on votes. If you play 10 rounds per hour, that's 50 to 150 stars per hour. Daily tasks can give an extra 50 to 100 stars. With consistent play, you can earn 500 to 1,000 stars in a week.

## FAQ

### How many stars do I need to unlock all items?

As of 2026, there are over 1,000 items in DTI. Unlocking everything costs roughly 50,000 stars. Focus on buying items you use often first.

### Can I farm stars while AFK?

No, you must be active to vote and participate. AFK players get no stars and may be kicked from servers.

### What is the best time to play for more votes?

Evenings and weekends have more active players. Try playing between 6 PM and 10 PM in your timezone for fuller servers.

### Do premium passes increase star earnings?

Yes, the Premium Pass gives a 20% star boost on all earnings. It costs $9.99 per month and is worth it if you play daily.

## The Bottom Line

Farming stars in DTI is about playing smart, not just playing a lot. Vote consistently, complete daily tasks, and use themed outfits to maximize votes. With these strategies, you can earn enough stars to unlock the items you want within a few weeks. Start today, and you'll see your star count climb.
