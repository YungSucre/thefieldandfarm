If you are looking for the best garlic chicken in Honolulu, you want the crispy, savory, garlicky plates that locals line up for. The top spots are Rainbow Drive-In, L&L Hawaiian Barbecue, and a few hidden gems like Yummy's and The Hibachi Stand. Each has its own take, but all deliver that signature Hawaiian garlic chicken: fried, coated in a sticky garlic sauce, and served over rice. Here is where to go and what to expect.

## What makes Hawaiian garlic chicken different

Hawaiian garlic chicken is not your average garlic chicken. It is typically boneless, skin-on chicken thighs, marinated in soy sauce, ginger, and lots of garlic, then deep-fried until crispy. The sauce is a sweet and savory glaze with minced garlic, often thickened with sugar or honey. It is served over steamed white rice, sometimes with mac salad. The result is a plate lunch staple that is both indulgent and satisfying.

## Top spots for garlic chicken in Honolulu

### Rainbow Drive-In

Rainbow Drive-In is a Honolulu institution, open since 1961. Their garlic chicken is a crowd favorite, featuring crispy fried chicken pieces tossed in a garlicky soy glaze. It comes as a plate lunch with rice and your choice of mac salad or tossed greens. Expect a line at lunch, but it moves fast. Price: around $12 to $14 for a regular plate.

### L&L Hawaiian Barbecue

L&L has multiple locations across the island, and their garlic chicken is consistent and reliable. It is similar to Rainbow's, with a slightly sweeter sauce. You can get it as a plate, a mini plate, or even a burrito. Prices range from $10 to $13 depending on size. L&L is a good choice if you want quick service and a familiar taste.

### Yummy's

Yummy's is a local favorite, known for its generous portions and bold garlic flavor. Their garlic chicken is less sweet, more garlic-forward, and the chicken is extra crispy. It is a bit off the tourist path, but worth the trip. Plate lunch with rice and mac salad runs about $13 to $15.

### The Hibachi Stand

A newer spot, The Hibachi Stand, offers a fusion take with garlic chicken that is grilled rather than fried. It is lighter, with a charred garlic glaze. They serve it over rice with a side of garlic butter vegetables. Prices are around $14 to $16. If you want a slightly healthier option, this is your pick.

## How to choose

If you want the classic, no-frills plate lunch, go to Rainbow Drive-In. If you want consistency and convenience, L&L is your best bet. For a local, off-the-beaten-path experience with big flavor, try Yummy's. And if you prefer grilled over fried, The Hibachi Stand is the modern twist.

## What to order and how to eat it

When you order garlic chicken, you typically get a plate with two scoops of rice and a side. Mac salad is the traditional choice, but you can often substitute with green salad or vegetables. Eat it hot, right away, so the chicken stays crispy. Use a fork and knife, or go local and use a spoon to scoop the rice and chicken together. Some places offer a "mix plate" if you want to try other meats too.

## Making it at home

If you cannot make it to Honolulu, you can recreate a decent version at home. Use boneless, skin-on chicken thighs, marinate in soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and a bit of sugar for at least 30 minutes. Dredge in cornstarch and fry until golden and crispy. For the sauce, sauté minced garlic in oil, add soy sauce, sugar, and a splash of water, and thicken with cornstarch slurry. Toss the fried chicken in the sauce and serve over rice. It will not be exactly like the plate lunch spots, but it is close.

## Prices and portions

In 2026, expect to pay $10 to $16 for a garlic chicken plate in Honolulu. Portions are generous, usually enough for two meals if you are not a big eater. Some places offer a "mini" size for around $8 to $9, which is perfect for a lighter lunch.

## FAQ

### Is garlic chicken spicy?

No, it is not spicy. The sauce is sweet and savory, with no heat. If you want spice, ask for chili pepper water or sriracha on the side.

### Can I get garlic chicken without rice?

Yes, most places will let you substitute the rice with extra vegetables or a salad, but you might pay a small fee. The rice is a big part of the plate, so consider trying it as is first.

### Are there vegetarian options?

Some spots offer tofu or vegetable plates, but garlic chicken is meat-based. If you are vegetarian, look for a separate menu item, as the sauce may contain fish sauce or oyster sauce.

### What is the best time to go?

Lunch rush is 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM, so lines are longest then. Go before 11 AM or after 2 PM for a shorter wait. Dinner is usually less crowded, but some places close by 8 PM.

## The bottom line

The best garlic chicken in Honolulu depends on your taste: classic and iconic, go to Rainbow Drive-In; reliable and convenient, L&L; bold and local, Yummy's; grilled and modern, The Hibachi Stand. Prices are fair, portions are big, and the flavor is unmistakably Hawaiian. Try one, or try them all, and you will see why this dish is a local treasure.
