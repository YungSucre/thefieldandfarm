If you're looking for the best corn maze in Maryland, start with these five farms: Summers Farm (Frederick), Weber's Farm (Parkville), Sharp's at Waterford Farm (Brookeville), Larriland Farm (Woodbine), and Gaver Farm (Mount Airy). Each offers a unique maze experience, with ticket prices ranging from $10 to $18 per person, and they typically open from late September through early November. This guide breaks down what each farm offers, when to go, and how to make the most of your visit.

![Best corn maze in maryland](/images/articles/best-corn-maze-in-maryland/section-1.jpg)

## Top Maryland Corn Mazes for 2026

### Summers Farm (Frederick, MD)
- **Maze size**: 8 acres, with a 3-acre children's maze
- **2026 dates**: September 20 through November 2, open daily 10 AM to 6 PM
- **Tickets**: $14.95 for adults, $12.95 for children (ages 3-12), free for under 3
- **Highlights**: Known for its elaborate maze design (2026 theme: "Maryland Pride"), plus pumpkin patch, corn cannon, and weekend hayrides. It's the largest maze in the region, so allow 2-3 hours to complete.

### Weber's Farm (Parkville, MD)
- **Maze size**: 5 acres, with a 1-acre kids' maze
- **2026 dates**: September 27 through November 1, weekends only (Sat-Sun, 10 AM to 5 PM)
- **Tickets**: $12 for adults, $10 for children (ages 2-12), free for under 2
- **Highlights**: A family-run farm with a maze shaped like a crab (a Maryland icon), plus a petting zoo, corn crib, and apple cider donuts. The maze is moderately challenging, with checkpoints to guide you.

### Sharp's at Waterford Farm (Brookeville, MD)
- **Maze size**: 6 acres, with a 2-acre maze for kids
- **2026 dates**: October 4 through November 2, open daily 9 AM to 5 PM
- **Tickets**: $15 for adults, $13 for children (ages 3-12), free for under 3
- **Highlights**: This farm focuses on education: you'll find QR codes at checkpoints that share farming facts. The maze is designed as a map of Maryland, and they offer a twilight maze on Friday and Saturday nights (until 8 PM) for $18.

### Larriland Farm (Woodbine, MD)
- **Maze size**: 4 acres, with a 1-acre children's maze
- **2026 dates**: September 20 through November 2, open daily 10 AM to 6 PM
- **Tickets**: $12 for adults, $10 for children (ages 4-12), free for under 4
- **Highlights**: Part of a larger pick-your-own farm, so you can combine maze time with apple and pumpkin picking. The maze is less crowded on weekdays, and they offer a discount for groups of 15 or more ($10 per person).

### Gaver Farm (Mount Airy, MD)
- **Maze size**: 3 acres, with a 1-acre kids' maze
- **2026 dates**: September 27 through November 2, open daily 10 AM to 6 PM
- **Tickets**: $10 for adults, $8 for children (ages 3-12), free for under 3
- **Highlights**: The most budget-friendly option, with a maze that includes a scavenger hunt for kids.

They also have a corn kernel pit, hay bale pyramid, and a farm market with local produce.

## How to Choose the Right Maze for You

Consider your group's needs:
- **For families with young children**: Choose Weber's Farm or Gaver Farm, as they have smaller mazes and more kid-focused activities.
- **For a challenging experience**: Summers Farm or Sharp's at Waterford Farm offer larger, more complex mazes that take over an hour to complete.
- **For a full day out**: Larriland Farm combines the maze with pick-your-own apples and pumpkins, making it a one-stop trip.
- **For budget-conscious visitors**: Gaver Farm has the lowest ticket prices, and you can often find coupons on their website or local coupon books.

## Planning Your Visit: Practical Tips

- **Check the weather**: Corn mazes are muddy after rain, so wear boots and old clothes. Most farms close the maze during heavy storms, so call ahead if rain is forecast.
- **Go early or late**: On weekends, arrive at opening time (10 AM) to avoid crowds. Late afternoon (after 3 PM) is also quieter, but you'll have less time before closing.
- **Bring cash**: While most farms accept cards, some smaller operations (like Gaver Farm) prefer cash. ATMs on site often charge fees.
- **Allow 2-3 hours**: That includes the maze, activities, and a snack break. Don't rush; the maze is the main event.
- **Use the checkpoints**: Most mazes have colored flags or punch cards. Use them to track your progress, and don't be afraid to ask for a hint; staff are usually nearby.

## What to Expect in 2026: Prices and Trends

Ticket prices have risen slightly from 2025, reflecting increased costs for seed, fuel, and labor. Expect to pay:
- **Adults**: $12 to $15
- **Children (3-12)**: $8 to $13
- **Group discounts**: Often available for 15+ people, saving $2 to $4 per ticket.

Many farms now offer online ticket sales, which can save you time but may include a small processing fee (around $1 per ticket). Also, some farms have added evening "flashlight mazes" (typically $18 to $22 per person) for a different experience.

## FAQ

**Q: When is the best time to visit a corn maze in Maryland?**
A: Late September to mid-October is ideal: the corn is tall, the weather is cool, and the crowds are thinner than in late October. If you want Halloween-themed activities, visit the last two weekends of October, but expect larger crowds.

**Q: Are corn mazes accessible for strollers or wheelchairs?**
A: Most mazes have paths wide enough for strollers, but the ground can be uneven. Weber's Farm and Gaver Farm are the most accessible, with packed dirt paths. For wheelchairs, call ahead to ask about surface conditions; some farms offer a separate, shorter route.

**Q: Can I bring my dog?**
A: Generally, no. Most farms do not allow pets due to health regulations and safety around farm animals. Service animals are permitted. Check each farm's policy online.

**Q: Do I need to buy tickets in advance?**
A: It's recommended for weekends in October, as some farms sell out. Online tickets are available for Summers Farm and Sharp's at Waterford Farm. For weekdays, you can usually buy at the gate.

## The Bottom Line

The best corn maze in Maryland depends on your group's size, age, and budget. For a classic, large maze with lots of activities, Summers Farm is your best bet. For a budget-friendly, family-focused outing, Gaver Farm delivers. If you want a unique theme and education, Sharp's at Waterford Farm stands out. Whichever you choose, plan to spend at least half a day, wear comfortable shoes, and bring cash for snacks. Check the farm's website before you go, as dates and prices can change. Enjoy the maze, and don't forget to find your way out!
