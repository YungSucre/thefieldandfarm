Fallen stars are a key resource in Terraria for crafting mana crystals and star cannon ammo. To farm them efficiently, you need a long, flat sky bridge above the surface, ideally around 200 feet up, and you need to walk or run across it at night to collect stars as they fall. This guide covers the exact setup, expected yields, and practical tips to get the most out of your star farming.

## Why farm stars?

Stars are used to craft mana crystals, which increase your maximum mana, and star cannon ammo, which is powerful early on. They also sell for a decent price, so farming them can be a steady source of coins. In

## Building the sky bridge

The core of star farming is a sky bridge: a long, flat platform high above the ground. Here is how to build one:

1. **Height**: Build the bridge at least 200 feet above the surface, but below the space layer (which starts at 300 feet in small worlds, higher in larger ones). A good rule is to build it around 250 feet up. You can use a depth meter to check your altitude.
2. **Materials**: Use any solid block, such as dirt, stone, or wood. Wood is cheap and easy to gather. You will need about 1000 blocks for a 200-block-long bridge, which is a good starting length.
3. **Length**: A longer bridge means more stars, but even 200 blocks is enough to see a steady trickle. For serious farming, aim for 500 to 1000 blocks. This will take time, but you can build it in sections.
4. **Surface**: Make the bridge completely flat and at least 2 blocks wide. You will be running along it, so a 3-block width is more comfortable and prevents falls.
5. **Access**: Add a rope or a set of platforms at one end so you can climb up quickly. Also, consider a small hut with a bed and a chest at the start to store stars and skip time.

## When and how to collect

Stars fall only at night, from 7:30 PM to 4:30 AM in game time. They land on any surface that is in the sky, so your bridge will catch them. Here is the collection routine:

- **Start at dusk**: Get to your bridge just before 7:30 PM. You can use a bed to sleep until night if you are early.
- **Walk or run**: Simply walk or run across the bridge from one end to the other. Stars will appear as glowing items on the platform. Pick them up as you go.
- **Repeat**: Once you reach the end, turn around and go back. Keep doing this until dawn. You will collect stars as they fall, but some may land behind you, so check the whole bridge periodically.
- **Use accessories**: Equip items that increase movement speed, like Hermes Boots or the Sailfish Boots, to cover more ground faster. A Minecart track on the bridge can also speed things up, but walking is fine for a start.

## Expected yields and rates

In a single night, on a 200-block bridge, you might collect 5 to 10 stars. On a 1000-block bridge, you can get 20 to 30 stars per night. This varies based on world size and luck, but those are realistic numbers. Over a full in-game week (about 2.5 real hours), you can expect around 150 to 200 stars on a large bridge. That is enough for 10 to 15 mana crystals, or a good stock of star cannon ammo.

## Tools and costs

You do not need special tools, but these help:

- **Depth meter**: To verify your height. Costs about 1 gold from the Merchant, or you can find one from enemies.
- **Hermes Boots**: Found in underground chests, they let you run faster. Free if you find them.
- **Minecart and track**: A minecart with track costs about 50 silver per 100 blocks from the Mechanic. This is optional but speeds up collection.
- **Bed**: Crafted from wood and silk, costs almost nothing. Essential for skipping time.

Total cost for a basic setup: under 2 gold coins, mostly for the depth meter and track if you choose to use it.

## Optimizing your farm

To get more stars per night, consider these tweaks:

- **Build higher**: Stars fall from the sky, so a bridge at 300 feet may catch more than one at 200 feet, but the difference is small. Test both heights if you can.
- **Remove background walls**: If you have any walls behind the bridge, they can block stars. Keep the area open.
- **Use a battle potion**: Not directly related to stars, but if you are also farming enemies for other drops, it helps. For stars alone, it does nothing.
- **Set up a timer**: Some players use a timer and a teleporter to automatically traverse the bridge, but that is advanced and requires wiring. For most, manual collection is fine.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Building too low**: If the bridge is below 150 feet, stars may land on the ground instead. Always go above 200 feet.
- **Building in the space layer**: If you go too high (above 300 feet in small worlds), you enter space, and stars still fall, but you might encounter harpies and other enemies. Keep it just below that.
- **Not clearing the area**: Trees or hills near the bridge can catch stars. Make sure the bridge is in open air.
- **Forgetting to collect**: Stars disappear at dawn, so if you miss them, they are gone. Always collect before 4:30 AM.

## FAQ

**Can I farm stars during the day?** No, stars only fall at night. You can use a bed to sleep until night, but you cannot collect during the day.

**Do stars fall on any surface?** They fall on any solid block that is in the sky, including platforms, but they do not fall on background walls. Your bridge must be made of solid blocks.

**How many stars do I need for a mana crystal?** You need 3 fallen stars to craft one mana crystal at a crystal ball or a demon altar. So 30 stars give you 10 mana crystals.

**Is star farming worth it in hardmode?** Yes, stars are still useful for star cannon ammo, which is effective against the Destroyer and other mechanical bosses. The farming method is the same.

## The bottom line

Star farming is simple and low-cost. Build a long, flat bridge at 200 to 250 feet, run across it at night, and collect stars. A 500-block bridge gives you a solid return, and you can expand it later. Start with a small bridge this week, and you will have a steady supply of stars for mana and ammo. No fancy gear required, just time and a bit of building.
