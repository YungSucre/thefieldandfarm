Star metal is one of the most valuable resources in Conan Exiles, used for top-tier weapons and tools. To farm it, you need to locate meteor showers, mine the fallen meteors with a pick, and smelt the ore in a furnace. Here is exactly how to do it, step by step.

## What You Need Before You Start

Before you head out to gather star metal, make sure you have the right gear and stats. You will need:

- A **steel pick** or better (a starmetal pick is ideal, but you need star metal to make it, so start with steel).
- **Carry weight** of at least 200, or bring a pack animal (a horse or bearer) to haul ore.
- **Cold protection**: star metal falls in the frozen north, so wear insulated armor or carry firewater.
- A **repair hammer** and materials to fix your pick on the go.
- A **furnace** and fuel (wood or coal) at your base to smelt the ore.

If you are below level 30, you will struggle to survive in the north. Level up first by hunting and crafting.

## Step 1: Find Meteor Showers

Star metal only appears after a meteor shower. These happen randomly, but you can predict them by watching the sky at night. Look for streaks of light, then head toward the impact site. The meteors land in the **northern part of the map**, specifically in the **Snow Biome**, around coordinates **L-6 to M-8** (on the standard map).

You can also check the **Event Log** in your menu (if enabled on your server) for meteor shower announcements. On official servers, showers occur every few hours, but timing varies.

## Step 2: Locate the Fallen Meteors

After a shower, the meteors appear as large, dark rocks with a blue glow. They are scattered across the snow, often in clusters. Use a **pick** to mine them. Each meteor yields **star metal ore** and sometimes **crystal** or **ironstone**.

Important: If you see a meteor that is still smoking, wait a few minutes. It may be too hot to mine, and you will take damage.

## Step 3: Mine the Ore

Equip your pick and attack the meteor. Each hit yields 1-3 ore. A single meteor can give 20-50 ore, depending on its size. Use **heavy attacks** (hold the attack button) to mine faster, but watch your stamina.

Your pick will take durability damage. Carry a repair hammer and repair it on the spot, or bring a second pick. If you are using a steel pick, expect to repair it twice per meteor cluster.

## Step 4: Smelt the Ore

Return to your base and place the star metal ore in a **furnace** with fuel. Each ore takes **2 minutes** to smelt into **1 star metal bar**. You need **2 ore per bar**. So, 100 ore gives you 50 bars.

Fuel: Use **wood** (burns 30 seconds per piece) or **coal** (burns 2 minutes). For a large batch, coal is more efficient. A furnace can hold 20 ore at a time, so plan accordingly.

## Step 5: Use the Bars

Star metal bars are used to craft **starmetal tools and weapons** at a **blacksmith's bench**. You also need **steel bars** and **shaped wood** for most recipes. For example, a **starmetal pick** requires 10 bars, 5 steel bars, and 5 shaped wood.

## Realistic Yields and Time

In a typical meteor shower, you can find 3-5 meteors. Each yields 20-50 ore, so you might get 60-250 ore per shower. That translates to 30-125 bars. Smelting takes 2 minutes per ore, so 100 ore takes 200 minutes (about 3.3 hours) in a single furnace. Build multiple furnaces to speed this up.

Prices on player-run servers (2026) vary: star metal ore sells for 1-3 gold per piece, and bars for 5-10 gold. On official servers, there is no currency, but you can trade.

## Tips for Efficient Farming

- **Build a small outpost** near the meteor zone with a furnace and repair station. This saves travel time.
- **Use a horse or bearer** to carry ore. A horse can carry 200 weight, a bearer 300.
- **Team up**: one player mines, another guards against wildlife (wolves, sabretooths, and yetis are common).
- **Check the sky at night**: if you see a meteor shower, mark the location on your map immediately.
- **Server settings**: on some private servers, meteor showers are more frequent or announced in chat. Adjust your strategy accordingly.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I find star metal without a meteor shower?**
A: No. Star metal only appears from meteor impacts. There are no static nodes. You must wait for a shower.

**Q: What is the best pick for mining star metal?**
A: A **starmetal pick** is best, but you need star metal to craft it. Start with a **steel pick** (requires a blacksmith's bench and steel bars). A hardened steel pick is a good middle ground.

**Q: How do I survive the cold while farming?**
A: Wear **insulated armor** (like the Hyborian or Vanir sets) or drink **firewater** (aloe, plant fiber, and water in a firebowl cauldron). Also, build a campfire nearby to warm up.

**Q: Do meteors respawn?**
A: Meteors do not respawn in the same spot. New showers create new meteors in different locations. The area stays active, but you must find fresh impacts.

## The Bottom Line

Farming star metal is a high-risk, high-reward activity. You need to be at least level 30, have a steel pick, and be ready to brave the frozen north. Watch the skies, mine fast, and smelt in bulk. With a good route and a pack animal, you can gather 100+ bars in a single shower, enough for a full set of starmetal tools. It is not a quick task, but it is the only way to get the best gear in the game. Plan your trip, bring repairs, and do not go alone.
