If you are looking for the best corn maze in Wisconsin, you want a farm that combines a challenging maze with family-friendly activities and good value. Based on size, design, and visitor experience, the top picks for 2026 are Treinen Farm Corn Maze & Pumpkin Patch in Lodi, Schuster's Farm in Deerfield, and County Line Orchard in Hobart. Each offers a unique experience, but Treinen Farm stands out for its massive 25-acre maze and intricate designs. Below, I break down what each farm offers, plus practical tips for planning your visit.

## Top Corn Mazes in Wisconsin

### Treinen Farm Corn Maze & Pumpkin Patch (Lodi)
- **Location:** W6741 Quality Dr, Lodi, WI 53555
- **Maze size:** 25 acres, one of the largest in the state
- **2026 theme:** "Wisconsin Badgers" (expected, based on past themes)
- **Prices:** $14-$18 per person (ages 3 and up); children under 3 free
- **Season:** Mid-September through October 31, open daily 10 AM to 7 PM (hours vary)
- **Highlights:** 5 miles of trails, 8-story observation tower, corn cannon, pumpkin patch, and hayrides. The maze is divided into sections, so you can do a shorter route if you have young kids.
- **Why it's a top pick:** The sheer size and the quality of the design make it a must-visit. The observation tower gives you a great view to plan your route, and the staff are friendly.

### Schuster's Farm (Deerfield)
- **Location:** W2652 US-12, Deerfield, WI 53531
- **Maze size:** 12 acres
- **2026 theme:** "Farm Life" (rotates annually)
- **Prices:** $12-$16 per person; combo tickets with other activities available
- **Season:** Late September to October 31, open weekends and some weekdays
- **Highlights:** 3-acre pumpkin patch, corn pit, jumping pillow, pedal carts, and a haunted maze on select nights. The farm also has a bakery with fresh cider donuts.
- **Why it's a top pick:** Great for families with young children because the maze is not overwhelming, and there are plenty of non-maze activities to keep everyone entertained.

### County Line Orchard (Hobart)
- **Location:** 2033 County Rd U, Hobart, WI 54155
- **Maze size:** 8 acres
- **2026 theme:** "Apple Harvest" (since it's also an orchard)
- **Prices:** $10-$14 per person; children under 2 free
- **Season:** Mid-September to October 31, open daily 9 AM to 6 PM
- **Highlights:** Apple picking (additional cost), hayrides, petting zoo, and a corn maze that is challenging but not too long. The orchard setting is beautiful in the fall.
- **Why it's a top pick:** If you want to combine apple picking with a

## How to Choose the Right Maze for You

Consider the age of your group and what you want out of the visit:

- **For a challenge:** Treinen Farm's 25-acre maze will test your navigation skills. Bring a map (they provide one) and plan to spend 2-3 hours inside.
- **For young children (under 8):** Schuster's Farm is more manageable. The maze has checkpoints with trivia questions, and you can easily exit if needed.
- **For a full day out:** County Line Orchard offers apple picking plus the maze, so you can make a day of it. Pack a picnic.

## Practical Tips for Your Visit

1. **Check the weather:** Corn mazes are muddy after rain. Wear boots and old clothes. Most farms are open rain or shine, but some activities may close in severe weather.
2. **Go early:** Arrive at opening time to beat the crowds, especially on weekends. The maze is easier to navigate when there are fewer people.
3. **Bring cash:** Some farms have ATMs, but they charge fees. Cash is faster for entry and for buying snacks.
4. **Wear comfortable shoes:** You will walk a lot. At Treinen, the full maze is 5 miles of trails.
5. **Take a photo of the maze map:** If you lose your paper map, you can use your phone. But note that cell service can be spotty in rural areas.

## What to Expect in 2026

Prices have risen slightly from 2025, about $1-$2 per ticket, due to increased costs for seed, fuel, and labor. Most farms now offer online ticket purchase, which can save you $1-$2 per ticket and let you skip the line. Check each farm's website for specific dates and times, as they can change.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are corn mazes suitable for toddlers?

Yes, but choose a smaller maze like Schuster's Farm. They have a separate mini-maze for kids under 5. Always keep an eye on little ones, as corn stalks can be taller than them.

### Can I bring my dog?

Most farms do not allow pets, except service animals. Leave your dog at home to avoid disappointment.

### How long does it take to complete a corn maze?

At Treinen, plan 1.5 to 3 hours. At Schuster's, 45 minutes to 1.5 hours. County Line takes about 1 hour. If you have a time limit, ask for the shortcut route.

### Do these farms offer group discounts?

Yes, most offer discounts for groups of 15 or more. Contact them in advance to arrange. You may need to book a specific time slot.

## The Bottom Line

For the best overall corn maze experience in Wisconsin, Treinen Farm is the winner due to its size and design. However, if you have young kids or want a more relaxed visit, Schuster's Farm or County Line Orchard are excellent choices. Whichever you pick, go on a weekday if possible, wear good shoes, and bring cash. Check the farm's website before you go for the latest hours and any weather-related closures. Enjoy the fall!
