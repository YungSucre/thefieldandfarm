## Top Corn Mazes in Colorado for 2026

### 1. Maize in the City (Denver)

Located just east of downtown Denver, Maize in the City is a massive 20-acre maze with a new design each year. In 2026, the theme is "Colorado Wildlife," featuring a mountain lion and bighorn sheep pattern. The maze takes about 1.5 to 2 hours to complete, and they offer flashlight nights on Fridays and Saturdays in October. Prices are $15 for adults, $12 for kids 4-12, and free for under 4. They also have a corn cannon and a jumping pillow for extra fun.

### 2. Anderson Farms (Erie)

Anderson Farms, about 30 minutes north of Denver, is a working farm that hosts a 15-acre maze. Their 2026 design is a tribute to Colorado agriculture, with a tractor and wheat sheaf pattern. The maze is known for its "Maze Map" app that helps you find checkpoints, and they have a haunted maze on select October nights. Admission is $18 for adults, $14 for kids, and includes access to the pumpkin patch and corn pit. They open September 20 through October 31, 2026.

### 3. Fritzler Corn Maze (LaSalle)

Fritzler Corn Maze, near Greeley, is one of the largest in the state at 25 acres. For 2026, the design is a tribute to the Colorado Rockies baseball team, with a baseball and bat pattern. This maze is challenging, with multiple bridges and a trivia game that adds a learning element. They also have a 5K run through the maze on October 10. Prices are $16 for adults, $13 for kids, with a family pass for $60. They open September 13 through November 1, 2026.

### 4. The Corn Maze at the Farm (Fort Collins)

This family-owned farm offers a 10-acre maze with a simpler design, perfect for younger children. The 2026 theme is "Farm Animals," with a pig and chicken pattern. They have a petting zoo and a hay maze for toddlers. Admission is $12 for everyone over 2, and they offer a discount for groups of 10 or more. Open September 20 through October 26, 2026.

## Planning Your Visit: Dates, Prices, and Tips

Most Colorado corn mazes open in mid-September and run through October, with some extending into early November. Peak times are weekends, especially afternoons, so go early or on a weekday to avoid crowds. Prices range from $12 to $18 per person, with family passes often saving you money. Always check the farm's website for up-to-date hours and weather closures, as rain can make paths muddy.

Here are practical tips for a smooth visit:

- Wear sturdy shoes; corn fields can be uneven and dusty.
- Bring water, especially on warm days, as mazes can take over an hour.
- Use the map or app provided; getting lost is fun, but not for hours.
- If you have young kids, stick to simpler mazes or use the "easy exit" flags.
- Arrive at least 2 hours before closing to have enough time.

## Beyond the Maze: Extra Attractions

Many farms offer more than just the maze. Look for pumpkin patches, hayrides, corn cannons, and farm animals. For example, Anderson Farms has a giant slide and a corn kernel sandbox, while Fritzler has a zip line and a mining sluice. These add-ons can make the trip worth the drive, especially for families. Check each farm's website for a full list of activities and any additional fees.

## FAQ

### When is the best time to visit a corn maze in Colorado?

Late September to mid-October is ideal, as the corn is fully grown and the weather is usually mild. Weekday mornings are the least crowded, and flashlight nights in October offer a different experience.

### Are corn mazes accessible for strollers or wheelchairs?

Most mazes have uneven ground, so strollers and wheelchairs can be difficult. Some farms, like Anderson Farms, have a paved path around the maze, but not through it. Call ahead to ask about accessibility options.

### Can I bring my dog to a corn maze?

Policies vary. Many farms allow well-behaved dogs on leashes, but some do not, especially during haunted events. Check the farm's website or call before you go.

### Do I need to buy tickets in advance?

It's recommended, especially for weekends and special events. Many farms sell out, and online tickets are often cheaper. Look for discounts on family passes or group rates.
