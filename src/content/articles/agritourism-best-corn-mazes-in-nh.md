The top spots combine challenging paths, family-friendly activities, and good value. Here are the standout corn mazes in NH for 2026, based on size, creativity, and overall experience.

## Top Corn Mazes in New Hampshire

### 1. Coppal House Farm (Lee, NH)

**What makes it special:** One of the largest corn mazes in New England, with a new design each year. The maze covers about 10 acres, and the farm also offers hayrides, a pumpkin patch, and farm animals.

**Details:** Open weekends from late September through October. Admission is $12 for adults, $8 for children (ages 4-12), and free for kids under 3. Expect to spend 1.5 to 2 hours inside the maze. They provide maps and checkpoints to keep you on track.

**Why it's a top pick:** The sheer size and the quality of the design make it a must-visit. The farm is well-organized, and the staff are friendly.

### 2. Beaver Brook Farm (Litchfield, NH)

**What makes it special:** A 5-acre maze with a fall festival vibe. Includes a corn pit, pedal carts, and a haunted maze on select nights.

**Details:** Open daily in October. Daytime admission is $10 per person; haunted maze tickets are $15. They also have a farm store with local produce and baked goods.

**Why it's a top pick:** Great for families with younger children because the maze is not overwhelming, and there are plenty of non-maze activities.

### 3. Apple Hill Farm (Concord, NH)

**What makes it special:** A 6-acre maze with a scenic view of the Merrimack Valley. The farm also has apple picking, a corn cannon, and a petting zoo.

**Details:** Open weekends in September and October. Admission is $11 for adults, $7 for children (ages 3-12). The maze takes about an hour to complete. They have a snack bar with cider donuts.

**Why it's a top pick:** The combination of apple picking and the maze makes it a full-day outing. The views are a bonus.

### 4. The Corn Maize at Meadowbrook Farm (Epsom, NH)

**What makes it special:** A 4-acre maze with a unique theme each year, plus a giant slide and a corn box for kids.

**Details:** Open weekends from late September to October. Admission is $9 per person. They offer group rates for 10 or more.

## How to Choose the Right Maze for You

Consider your group's age and interest. If you have young kids, a smaller maze like Beaver Brook or Meadowbrook is less tiring. If you want a challenge, Coppal House is the way to go. Also, check if the maze is open after dark for a spooky experience, if that's your thing.

## Practical Tips for Visiting

- **Wear sturdy shoes:** The ground can be uneven, and you'll be walking on dirt and corn stalks.
- **Bring water:** Especially on warm days, you'll get thirsty.
- **Go early:** Arrive right at opening time to avoid crowds and have the maze to yourself.
- **Use the map:** Most mazes provide a map or have checkpoints. Don't rely on memory alone.
- **Check the weather:** Rain can make the paths muddy. Some mazes close in bad weather, so call ahead.

## What to Expect in 2026

Prices have held steady over the past few years, with most mazes charging between $9 and $12 per person. Some farms offer family passes or discounts for groups. Expect to spend about $30 to $40 for a family of four, not including food or extras.

## FAQ

**Q: When is the best time to visit a corn maze?**
A: Late September to mid-October is ideal. The corn is fully grown, and the weather is usually pleasant. Weekends are busiest, so go on a weekday if you can.

**Q: Are corn mazes suitable for young children?**
A: Yes, but choose a smaller maze and stay together. Many mazes have kid-friendly activities like corn pits and hayrides.

**Q: Can I bring my dog?**
A: Some mazes allow dogs on a leash, but many do not. Check the farm's website or call ahead.

**Q: How long does it take to complete a corn maze?**
A: It depends on the size and your navigation skills. Most people take 45 minutes to 2 hours. If you're lost, follow the paths to the nearest checkpoint.

## The Bottom Line

New Hampshire has excellent corn mazes for every type of visitor. Coppal House Farm offers the biggest challenge, while Beaver Brook and Meadowbrook are great for families. Plan your visit for a clear fall day, wear comfortable shoes, and bring cash for admission and treats. You'll have a memorable time exploring the twists and turns of these local farms.
