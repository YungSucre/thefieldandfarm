## Top Corn Mazes in Utah for 2026

Here are the top corn mazes in Utah, based on size, design, and visitor reviews.

### 1. Cornbelly's Corn Maze & Pumpkin Festival (Lehi)

Located at Thanksgiving Point, this is Utah's largest corn maze, covering over 20 acres. The maze design changes yearly, and 2026's theme is "Utah's Wild West." It's open from late September through October, daily from 10 AM to 10 PM. Admission is $16.95 for adults, $13.95 for kids 3-12, and free for under 3. Expect to spend 1.5 to 2 hours to complete the maze, but there's also a pumpkin patch, corn cannon, and giant slides.

### 2. Hee Haw Farms (Pleasant Grove)

Hee Haw Farms offers a 10-acre maze with a farm-themed design. It's open from September 25 to October 31, 2026, from 10 AM to 9 PM on weekdays and 9 AM to 10 PM on weekends. Admission is $14 for adults, $12 for kids 3-12, and free for under 3. The maze is well-marked, and they provide free maps. They also have a petting zoo and a corn kernel pit for younger kids.

### 3. The Corn Maze at Pack Farms (Payson)

Pack Farms has a 12-acre maze with a "Harvest Moon" theme for 2026. Open weekends only, from September 26 to October 31, 10 AM to 8 PM. Admission is $13 for adults, $11 for kids 3-12. They offer a flashlight maze on Friday and Saturday nights for an extra $3. The maze is challenging but has checkpoints with trivia questions, which kids love.

### 4. Black Island Farms (Syracuse)

Black Island Farms features a 15-acre maze with a "Space Adventure" theme. Open daily from September 19 to October 31, 9 AM to 9 PM. Admission is $15 for adults, $12 for kids 3-12. They have a corn maze app that tracks your progress, which is handy. Also on site: a giant jumping pillow and a corn box.

### 5. The Corn Maze at Wheeler Historic Farm (Murray)

This is a smaller, 5-acre maze, ideal for families with young children. Open weekends in October, 10 AM to 6 PM. Admission is $10 per person, free for under 2. The maze is less intense, and they have a scavenger hunt for kids. It's a good option if you want a quick visit.

## How to Choose the Right Maze for You

Consider your group's age and interest. For older kids and adults who want a challenge, Cornbelly's or Black Island are best. For families with toddlers, Wheeler Historic Farm is easier. If you want a full day of activities, Hee Haw Farms and Pack Farms offer more than just the maze.

## Tips for Visiting a Corn Maze

- **Go early**: Arrive right at opening to avoid crowds and have the maze to yourself.
- **Wear comfortable shoes**: You'll walk 1 to 2 miles, so sneakers are a must.
- **Bring water**: Especially on warm days, dehydration is a risk.
- **Use the map**: Most mazes provide maps, but you can also use your phone's GPS if you get lost.
- **Check the weather**: Rain can make paths muddy, so check the forecast and call ahead if storms are likely.

## What to Expect in 2026

Prices have risen slightly from 2025, but most mazes are still under $20 per person. Many offer online ticket discounts, so check their websites before you go. Also, expect longer lines on weekends, so consider a weekday visit if possible.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When do corn mazes open in Utah?

Most open in late September and run through October 31. Some, like Cornbelly's, open as early as September 19.

### Are corn mazes suitable for young children?

Yes, but choose a smaller maze like Wheeler Historic Farm. Larger mazes can be overwhelming for toddlers, but they often have kids' areas with activities.

### Can I bring my dog?

Generally, no. Most farms don't allow pets due to health and safety regulations. Service animals are usually permitted.

### Do I need to book tickets in advance?

It's recommended, especially for weekends. Many farms offer online tickets at a discount, and some have capacity limits.

## The Bottom Line

For the best corn maze experience in Utah, Cornbelly's is the top pick for size and variety, but Hee Haw Farms offers great value for families. Plan your visit for a weekday to avoid crowds, and always check the farm's website for the latest hours and prices. With a little planning, you'll have a memorable fall outing.
