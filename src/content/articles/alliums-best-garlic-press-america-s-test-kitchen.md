## What America's Test Kitchen Looks For

ATK evaluates garlic presses on several key criteria. You want a press that:

- **Crushes garlic thoroughly**: No big chunks left behind.
- **Requires moderate effort**: You shouldn't need a bodybuilder's grip.
- **Cleans easily**: Hinges and crevices trap garlic pulp, which is a pain.
- **Feels durable**: A flimsy press will bend or break within a season.

Their top pick has consistently been the Kuhn Rikon Epicurean, and for good reason. It uses a lever mechanism instead of a simple hinge, which gives you more leverage and reduces hand strain. ## How to Use a Garlic Press Like a Pro

Even the best press won't help if you use it wrong. Here's the method we've settled on after years of mistakes:

1. **Peel the clove first**: Pressing unpeeled garlic works, but you'll get bits of skin in your food. A quick smash with the flat of a knife loosens the skin easily.
2. **Cut large cloves**: If a clove is bigger than the chamber, cut it in half lengthwise. Don't force it.
3. **Press with steady pressure**: Squeeze firmly, not jerkily. You want a smooth extrusion.
4. **Clean immediately**: Garlic residue dries like cement. Rinse the press under hot water right after use, and use the included cleaner or a brush for the holes.

## Why Not Just Use a Knife?

A garlic press is faster than mincing by hand, and it releases more of the garlic's oils, which gives a stronger flavor. If you're processing several cloves for a recipe, the press saves time and keeps your fingers from smelling like garlic for hours. That said, a press is not for every dish. If you want thin slices for a stir-fry, use a knife. But for sauces, dressings, and marinades, a press is the tool.

## FAQ

### Is the Kuhn Rikon press worth the extra money?

Yes, if you use garlic often. The lever mechanism reduces hand strain, and the build quality means it will last for years. The OXO is fine for occasional use, but the Kuhn Rikon feels better and cleans easier.

### Can I put my garlic press in the dishwasher?

Most presses are not dishwasher safe, even if the manufacturer says they are. The high heat and detergent can warp the metal or damage the finish. Hand washing takes 30 seconds, so it's worth the effort.

### How do I get garlic out of the holes?

Rinse the press under hot water immediately after use, then use the included cleaning tool (if it has one) or a stiff brush. For stubborn bits, soak the press in hot soapy water for a few minutes.

### What's the best way to store a garlic press?

Keep it in a drawer with the handle closed, or hang it on a hook. Avoid stacking heavy items on top of it, as that can bend the hinge.
