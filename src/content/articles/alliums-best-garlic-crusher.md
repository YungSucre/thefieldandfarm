## What to look for in a garlic crusher

A good crusher must do three things: crush effectively, clean easily, and survive repeated use. Look for these features:

- **Material**: Solid stainless steel or cast aluminum. Avoid plastic hinges; they break. Zinc alloy is common but can bend under pressure.
- **Hopper design**: A removable hopper makes cleaning a breeze. Fixed hoppers trap pulp and are hard to rinse.
- **Leverage**: Longer handles mean more force with less effort. A curved handle fits your palm better.
- **Cleaning**: Some models come with a small brush or are dishwasher safe. I prefer hand washing; dishwashers can dull edges.
- **Capacity**: Standard presses hold one to two cloves. If you cook in bulk, look for a larger hopper or a rocker.

## How to use and care for your crusher

1. **Peel or not?** Most good presses handle unpeeled cloves, but peeling improves yield. To peel quickly, smash the clove with the flat side of a knife.
2. **Position**: Place the clove in the hopper, close the press, and squeeze firmly. Do not overfill; one clove at a time is best.
3. **Clean immediately**: Rinse under hot water right after use. Use the brush to clear holes. For stuck pulp, soak in warm soapy water for 10 minutes.
4. **Dry completely**: Moisture causes rust. Air dry or towel dry before storing.
5. **Avoid dishwashers**: Even if labeled dishwasher safe, the detergent can corrode metal over time.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use a garlic crusher for ginger or other roots?

Yes, most presses can handle small pieces of ginger, but they may leave fibrous strings. For ginger, a grater or microplane works better. Some crushers, like the Kuhn Rikon, come with a separate ginger plate, but it is an extra cost.

### How do I clean a garlic crusher with stuck pulp?

Rinse immediately, then use the included brush or a toothpick to clear holes. If pulp is dried, soak in hot soapy water for 10 minutes, then scrub. Avoid metal utensils that can scratch.

### Is a garlic press better than a knife?

For speed and ease, yes. A press gives a finer paste, which releases more allicin, the compound that gives garlic its health benefits. A knife gives a coarser chop, which is better for texture in stir-fries. Both have a place; I use a press for sauces and a knife for chunks.

### What is the best garlic crusher for arthritis or weak hands?

Look for a press with a long, ergonomic handle and a lever mechanism, like the Kuhn Rikon Easy Pull. It requires less grip strength. A rocker-style chopper is also easier, as it uses a rocking motion rather than squeezing.
