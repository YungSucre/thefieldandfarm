The short answer: for most healthy adults, 1 to 2 cloves of garlic per day is a safe and beneficial amount. That's roughly 1 to 2 grams of fresh garlic, or about 3 to 6 mg of allicin potential. Eating more can cause digestive upset or interact with medications. If you're growing your own, this guide will also help you plan how many bulbs to plant for a year's supply.

## Why 1 to 2 cloves?

Garlic's health benefits come from allicin, a compound released when cloves are crushed or chewed. Studies suggest that regular consumption of 1 to 2 cloves daily can support heart health, immune function, and blood pressure. ## How much garlic is in a clove?

A single clove of hardneck garlic averages 3 to 5 grams. Softneck cloves are slightly smaller, 2 to 4 grams. A typical bulb has 10 to 15 cloves, so 1 to 2 cloves per day means you'll use a bulb every 5 to 10 days.

## Daily intake by form

| Form | Amount per day | Notes |
|------|----------------|-------|
| Fresh raw garlic | 1 to 2 cloves (3-6 g) | Crush or chop, let sit 10 min before cooking to activate allicin |
| Cooked garlic | 2 to 3 cloves | Cooking reduces allicin, so slightly more is okay |
| Garlic powder | 1/2 to 1 teaspoon (1.5-3 g) | Lower allicin content |
| Aged garlic extract | 600 to 1,200 mg | Standard supplement dose |
| Garlic oil | 2 to 5 mg | Use as directed, not for internal use unless labeled |

## How much to grow for a year

If you eat 1 to 2 cloves daily, that's 365 to 730 cloves a year. With 10 to 15 cloves per bulb, you need about 25 to 50 bulbs. To account for storage losses and smaller bulbs, plant 50 to 75 bulbs. That's roughly 1 to 2 pounds of seed garlic. For a family of four, multiply by 3 or 4.

## Planting for a year's supply

- **Zones 3-5:** Plant in mid-October, 4-6 weeks before the ground freezes.
- **Zones 6-7:** Plant in late October to mid-November.
- **Zones 8-9:** Plant in November or early December.

Spacing: 6 inches apart in rows 12 inches apart. Plant cloves 2 to 3 inches deep, pointed side up. Mulch with 4 to 6 inches of straw for winter protection.

## Varieties that store well

For year-round supply, choose softneck types: they store 6 to 9 months. Hardneck varieties store 3 to 5 months, so plant a mix if you want both scapes and long storage.

| Variety | Type | Storage | Flavor |

| 'Inchelium Red' | Softneck | 8 months | Mild, sweet |
| 'Music' | Hardneck | 5 months | Robust, spicy |
| 'German Extra Hardy' | Hardneck | 5 months | Strong |

## Realistic costs for 2026

Seed garlic costs $8 to $15 per pound for common varieties, $20 to $30 per pound for gourmet types. A 50-bulb plot needs about 2 pounds of seed, so $16 to $60. A 100-square-foot bed yields 40 to 60 pounds of garlic, which is plenty for a year.

## How to eat that much garlic

Eating 1 to 2 cloves daily is easy if you incorporate it into meals:

- Add minced raw garlic to salad dressings, salsa, or avocado toast.
- Roast a whole bulb and spread on bread: roasting mellows the flavor.
- Stir-fry with vegetables: add garlic at the end to preserve some allicin.
- Make a quick garlic honey: cover peeled cloves with raw honey, let sit for 3 weeks, then take a spoonful daily.

## Side effects and precautions

- **Digestive upset:** If you're not used to garlic, start with half a clove and work up.
- **Bad breath:** Chew fresh parsley or apple after eating.
- **Medication interactions:** Garlic can thin the blood. If you take warfarin or other anticoagulants, check with your doctor.
- **Allergies:** Rare, but possible. Stop if you get a rash or swelling.

## FAQ

**Can I eat too much garlic?**

Yes. More than 3 to 4 cloves a day can cause heartburn, nausea, or diarrhea. Very high doses (10+ cloves) may lead to tissue damage. Stick to 1 to 2 cloves daily.

**Is raw garlic better than cooked?**

Raw garlic has more allicin, but it's harsh on the stomach. Cooking reduces allicin but still offers benefits. If you eat cooked garlic, use slightly more (2 to 3 cloves) to get similar effects.

**Can I take garlic supplements instead?**

Yes, but choose aged garlic extract or standardized allicin supplements. Follow the label dose, typically 600 to 1,200 mg daily. Supplements don't give you the same culinary benefits, but they're convenient.

**How do I store garlic for a year?**

Cure bulbs in a dry, shaded spot for 2 to 3 weeks, then trim tops and roots. Store in a cool (50-60°F), dry, dark place with good air circulation. Softneck braids can hang in the kitchen, but they'll sprout faster.

## The bottom line

Eat 1 to 2 cloves of fresh garlic daily for health benefits without side effects. To have that all year, plant 50 to 75 bulbs this fall, choose softneck varieties for storage, and store them properly. Start with a small patch, and adjust based on your family's taste. Garlic is one of the easiest crops for a homesteader, and the payoff is worth the effort.
