If you are wondering what it costs to stay at a castle farm, expect to pay between $250 and $600 per night for a double room in 2026, with weekly rates often discounted by 10 to 20 percent. The final price depends on the region, the season, the room type, and what is included, such as meals, farm tours, or castle access. Here is a breakdown of typical costs and how to plan your stay without surprises.

## What drives the price at a castle farm

Castle farms are a niche within agritourism, combining historic accommodations with working farm experiences. Their rates are higher than standard farm stays because of the upkeep of historic buildings, limited room counts, and the added value of castle amenities
![How much to stay at castle farm](/images/articles/how-much-to-stay-at-castle-farm/section-2.jpg)
. In 2026, you will pay a premium for the novelty, but you can find a range of budgets if you plan.

## Average nightly rates in 2026

Based on current listings across the United States and Europe, here are realistic price ranges for a castle farm stay:

| Room type | Low season (per night) | High season (per night) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic double room (shared bath) | $200 - $300 | $350 - $450 |
| Private double room with en-suite | $300 - $450 | $450 - $600 |
| Suite or family room | $400 - $600 | $600 - $800 |
| Entire cottage or tower (sleeps 4-6) | $500 - $800 | $800 - $1,200 |

These rates typically include breakfast, access to the farm grounds, and sometimes a guided tour. High season runs from May through October in most temperate regions, plus holiday weeks like Christmas and Easter. Low season is November through April, excluding holidays.

## What is included in the nightly rate

Most castle farms include the following in their base price:

- Overnight accommodation in a historic room
- Farm breakfast (often with eggs, produce, and dairy from the farm)
- Access to walking trails, gardens, and common areas
- Parking and Wi-Fi (though Wi-Fi can be spotty in thick stone walls)
- A basic farm tour or self-guided map

Extras that cost more: dinner (often $50 to $80 per person), cooking classes, horseback riding, spa services, and private castle tours. Some farms offer all-inclusive packages that bundle meals and activities, which can save you 10 to 15 percent compared to booking separately.

## How to estimate your total cost

To budget accurately, add up the following:

1. **Lodging**: Multiply the nightly rate by the number of nights. If you stay 5 nights or more, ask about a weekly rate, which often cuts 10 to 20 percent.
2. **Meals**: If you eat dinner at the farm most nights, budget $60 to $100 per person per day for food (breakfast is included).
3. **Activities**: Plan $30 to $100 per person for optional experiences like cheese-making workshops, archery, or vineyard tours.
4. **Transportation**: Castle farms are often rural, so factor in rental car costs or transfer fees. A rental car in the US runs $50 to $80 per day in 2026.
5. **Taxes and fees**: Expect 10 to 15 percent in local taxes and service charges on top of the quoted rate.

For a 3-night stay for two people in a private room with en-suite, mid-range, here is a sample budget:

- Lodging: 3 nights x $400 = $1,200
- Dinners for two: 3 x $120 = $360
- One activity for two: $100
- Transportation (gas or rental share): $100
- Taxes (12%): $144
- **Total: around $1,904**, or about $317 per person per night.

## Ways to save money on a castle farm stay

- **Travel in shoulder season**: May and September often have mild weather and lower rates than July and August. You can save 20 to 30 percent.
- **Book directly with the farm**: Use the farm's own website or call. You often get a 5 to 10 percent discount compared to booking through an online travel agency, and you can ask for a better room.
- **Stay longer**: Weekly rates are common. A 7-night stay often brings the per-night cost down to $300 or less for a private room.
- **Choose a smaller castle farm**: Properties with fewer than 10 rooms tend to be cheaper than large, famous castles. Look for "castle farm" or "manor farm" listings on agritourism sites.
- **Pack your own picnic**: If the farm allows, buy local produce and make your own lunch. You will save $20 to $30 per person per day.

## What to look for when booking

Before you reserve, ask these questions:

- What exactly is included in the nightly rate? Are taxes and fees listed?
- Is breakfast farm-sourced? Are there options for dietary restrictions?
- What is the cancellation policy? (Many require 7 to 14 days' notice for a refund.)
- Are there extra charges for using the pool, spa, or other amenities?
- Can you visit the farm animals and participate in daily chores? Some farms offer this for free, others charge $20 per person.

## Realistic expectations for the experience

A castle farm is not a luxury hotel. Rooms may have uneven floors, drafty windows, and antique furniture that is charming but not always comfortable. Wi-Fi can be weak, and there may be no TV. What you get instead is a unique atmosphere, home-cooked meals, and the chance to see a working farm up close. If you want modern amenities, look for a "castle hotel" instead, but expect to pay 50 percent more.

## FAQ

**Is it cheaper to stay at a castle farm in Europe or the US?**
European castle farms, especially in France, Italy, and Scotland, often range from $200 to $500 per night for a double room, similar to US rates. However, the US has fewer true castle farms, so competition is lower and prices can be higher in popular regions like the Hudson Valley or Napa. In 2026, you will find better value in Eastern Europe, where rates start around $150 per night.

**Can I visit a castle farm for a day without staying overnight?**
Many castle farms offer day tours or lunch packages for $30 to $60 per person. This is a good way to experience the property without the full cost of lodging. Check the farm's website for tour times, as they often require advance booking.

**Do castle farms offer discounts for children?**
Some do, especially if children stay in the same room as parents. You might pay an extra $20 to $40 per child for breakfast and a rollaway bed. A few farms have family packages that include activities like feeding animals, which can be a good deal.

**What is the best time of year to book for lower rates?**
The cheapest months are November through March, excluding holidays. However, many castle farms close during the coldest months. If you want to go in winter, look for farms in milder climates like California, Texas, or southern Italy. Otherwise, book in May or September for a balance of price and weather.

## The bottom line

Staying at a castle farm in 2026 will cost you between $250 and $600 per night for a private room, with all-inclusive packages and longer stays offering better value. To avoid surprises, always confirm what is included, ask about taxes, and book directly with the farm. If you plan ahead and travel in shoulder season, you can enjoy a memorable farm stay without breaking the bank.
