## Richardson Adventure Farm: The Giant

Richardson Adventure Farm holds the Guinness World Record for the largest corn maze, and 2026's design is a tribute to Illinois agriculture. The maze covers 28 acres with over 10 miles of paths. It takes most visitors 2 to 3 hours to complete the full maze, but there are shortcuts if you need to bail out.

- **Address**: 909 English Prairie Rd, Spring Grove, IL 60081
- **2026 Season**: Opens September 6, closes November 2 (weekends and some weekdays)
- **Admission**: $24 online, $28 at the gate. Kids 4-12 are $16, under 4 free.
- **Extras**: Hayrides, corn cannon, giant jumping pillow, and a 5-acre sunflower field.
- **Tip**: Go on a weekday morning to avoid crowds. The maze can take longer on weekends, and you might get lost for an extra hour.

This is the best choice if you want the biggest and most impressive maze. It's not the cheapest, but the scale is unmatched.

## Siegel's Cottonwood Farm: Best for Families

Siegel's Cottonwood Farm in Bolingbrook is the best all-around for families with young kids. The 8-acre maze has a shorter, easier path for little ones, and the farm offers a full day of activities without breaking the bank.

- **Address**: 17250 S Weber Rd, Bolingbrook, IL 60490
- **2026 Season**: September 13 through November 2, open daily
- **Admission**: $15 online, $18 at the gate. Kids 3-12 are $12, under 3 free.
- **Extras**: Pumpkin patch, corn pit, petting zoo, and a 3-acre sunflower maze.
- **Tip**: Buy tickets online to save $3 per person. Arrive before 10 AM to get a parking spot close to the entrance.

Siegel's is a safe bet for a fun, low-stress outing. The maze is challenging enough for adults but not overwhelming for kids.

## Bengtson's Pumpkin Farm: Haunted and Fun

If you want a corn maze with a spooky twist, Bengtson's in Homer Glen is your pick. They offer a daytime family maze and a separate haunted maze at night, which is not for young children.

- **Address**: 13451 W McCarthy Rd, Homer Glen, IL 60491
- **2026 Season**: September 20 through November 2, open daily
- **Admission**: Day maze $14 online, $17 at gate. Haunted maze $20 online, $24 at gate (ages 12+).
- **Extras**: Pumpkin patch, corn cannon, and a giant slide.
- **Tip**: The haunted maze sells out on Friday and Saturday nights. Book at least a week ahead.

Bengtson's is a great choice for teens and adults who want a thrill, but skip it if you have kids under 10.

## Kuiper's Family Farm: Scenic and Relaxed

Kuiper's Family Farm in Maple Park offers a 10-acre maze with beautiful views of the surrounding countryside. It's less crowded than the Chicago-area farms, making it a good option for a peaceful day out.

- **Address**: 1N350 Watson Rd, Maple Park, IL 60151
- **2026 Season**: September 13 through November 2, weekends only (plus some Fridays)
- **Admission**: $16 online, $20 at gate. Kids 3-12 are $12.
- **Extras**: Sunflower fields, hayrides, and a farm market with fresh produce.
- **Tip**: Check the farm's Facebook page for live updates on sunflower bloom status. Late September is usually peak.

Kuiper's is a hidden gem for those who want to avoid the crowds and enjoy the scenery.

## Goebbert's Pumpkin Patch: Budget Pick

Goebbert's in Pingree Grove is the most affordable option on this list, and it still offers a solid 6-acre maze. It's a working farm, so you get a more authentic agritourism experience.

- **Address**: 40W101 Il Route 72, Pingree Grove, IL 60140
- **2026 Season**: September 13 through November 2, open daily
- **Admission**: $12 online, $15 at gate. Kids 3-12 are $10.
- **Extras**: Pumpkin patch, farm animals, and a corn cannon.
- **Tip**: Combine your visit with a trip to the on-site bakery for fresh apple cider donuts.

Goebbert's is perfect if you're on a budget or want a low-key afternoon.

## How to Choose the Right Maze for You

Here's a quick decision guide:

- **For the biggest maze**: Richardson Adventure Farm. It's a once-in-a-lifetime experience.
- **For young kids**: Siegel's Cottonwood Farm. The maze is manageable, and there's plenty to do.
- **For a spooky night out**: Bengtson's Haunted Maze. Book ahead.
- **For a scenic, quiet day**: Kuiper's Family Farm. Go on a weekday if possible.
- **For a budget trip**: Goebbert's Pumpkin Patch. You'll still have fun.

## Practical Tips for Your Visit

- **Buy tickets online**: Most farms charge $3 to $5 more at the gate. Book at least a day ahead.
- **Wear sturdy shoes**: Corn mazes can be dusty and uneven. Sneakers or boots are best.
- **Bring water**: You'll be walking for 1 to 3 hours. Some farms have water stations, but not all.
- **Use the map**: Every maze has checkpoints with coded maps. Take a photo of the map with your phone so you don't lose it.
- **Go early**: Arrive at opening time to beat the crowds and the heat. Afternoon visits can be packed on weekends.
- **Check the weather**: Rain can turn the maze into mud. If it's been rainy, call ahead to see if the maze is open.

## FAQ

**What is the largest corn maze in Illinois?**
Richardson Adventure Farm in Spring Grove has a 28-acre maze, the largest in the world. It's open from early September to early November.

**When is the best time to visit a corn maze?**
Late September to mid-October is ideal. The corn is fully grown, and the weather is usually mild. Avoid the last weekend before Halloween if you want fewer crowds.

**Are corn mazes suitable for young children?**
Yes, most farms offer a shorter, easier path for kids. Siegel's and Goebbert's are especially family-friendly. Always stay with children under 10.

**Do I need to book tickets in advance?**
It's recommended, especially on weekends. Online tickets are cheaper and guarantee entry. Some haunted mazes sell out, so book a week ahead.

## The bottom line

The best corn maze in Illinois for most people is Richardson Adventure Farm if you want the biggest and most impressive, but Siegel's Cottonwood Farm is the best value for families. For a budget or a spooky twist, Goebbert's and Bengtson's are solid alternatives. No matter which you choose, buy tickets online, go early, and wear comfortable shoes. You'll have a memorable fall day without the stress.
