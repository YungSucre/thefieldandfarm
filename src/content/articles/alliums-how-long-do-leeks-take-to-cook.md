Leeks cook quickly: sliced leeks are tender in 5 to 10 minutes when sautéed or boiled, while whole or halved leeks need 15 to 20 minutes to soften through. The exact time depends on the cut, the cooking method, and how soft you want them. Here are the real numbers for each technique, plus what to do with them this week.

## Why leeks cook faster than onions

Leeks have thin, layered leaves and a high water content, so they break down faster than storage onions. That is why you can toss sliced leeks into a pan and have them ready in under 10 minutes. The white and light green parts are the most tender; the dark green tops are tougher and need longer cooking or are best saved for stock.

## Cooking times by method

Use these times as a starting point, then adjust for your stove and how soft you like them. All times assume medium heat for stovetop methods and 400°F for oven methods.

| Method | Cut | Time | Texture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Boiling | Sliced (1/4 inch) | 5 to 7 minutes | Tender, good for soups |
| Boiling | Whole or halved | 10 to 15 minutes | Soft, holds shape |
| Steaming | Sliced | 6 to 8 minutes | Crisp-tender |
| Steaming | Whole | 12 to 15 minutes | Fully tender |
| Sautéing | Sliced | 5 to 7 minutes | Golden edges, sweet |
| Sautéing | Half-moons | 8 to 10 minutes | Caramelized |
| Roasting | Halved lengthwise | 20 to 25 minutes | Soft, caramelized |
| Grilling | Halved lengthwise | 8 to 12 minutes | Charred, smoky |
| Braising | Whole or halved | 25 to 30 minutes | Very soft, silky |

## How to tell when leeks are done

Insert the tip of a paring knife into the thickest part. It should slide in with little resistance, like a ripe potato. For sliced leeks, taste one: it should be sweet and tender, not crunchy or fibrous. If you are cooking for a soup or stew, you may want them softer, so add 2 to 3 minutes.

## Step-by-step: perfect sautéed leeks

1. Trim the dark green tops and the root end. Cut the leek in half lengthwise, then slice crosswise into 1/4-inch half-moons.
2. Wash the slices in a bowl of cold water, swishing to release grit. Lift out with your hands and drain in a colander. Repeat if the water is sandy.
3. Heat 1 tablespoon of butter or olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the leeks and a pinch of salt.
4. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 5 to 7 minutes. They will soften and turn translucent. If they start to brown too fast, lower the heat.
5. Season and serve. They are ready when the edges are golden and the texture is silky.

## Common mistakes and fixes

- **Not washing well:** Leeks trap dirt between layers. Always wash after cutting, not before.
- **Cooking too long:** Leeks can turn mushy if boiled for 15 minutes or more. Check at the lower end of the time range.
- **Using only the white part:** The light green is just as tender and adds more flavor. Save the dark green for stock or compost.
- **High heat for sautéing:** Medium heat is enough. High heat burns the sugars before the inside softens.

## Recipes that use these times

- **Quick leek and potato soup:** Sauté sliced leeks for 5 minutes, add diced potatoes and broth, simmer for 20 minutes. The leeks are already tender.
- **Roasted leeks with lemon:** Halve leeks lengthwise, toss with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast at 400°F for 20 to 25 minutes, then squeeze lemon over the top.
- **Grilled leeks for a side dish:** Brush halved leeks with oil, grill over medium heat for 4 to 6 minutes per side. They should have char marks and be soft inside.

## FAQ

**Can you eat leeks raw?**
Yes, but they are tougher and more pungent than cooked. Slice them very thinly and soak in ice water for 10 minutes to crisp and mellow the flavor. Use in salads or as a garnish.

**Do leeks take longer than onions to cook?**
No, leeks cook faster. Sliced leeks are done in 5 to 7 minutes; sliced onions take 8 to 10 minutes to soften and 15 to 20 to caramelize.

**How do I know if leeks are overcooked?**
They become mushy and lose their structure. If you are making a pureed soup, that is fine, but for a side dish, pull them out when they are just tender.

**Can I cook leeks in the microwave?**
Yes. Place sliced leeks in a microwave-safe dish with 2 tablespoons of water, cover, and cook on high for 3 to 4 minutes. Stir once. They will be tender, though without browning.

## The bottom line

Leeks are one of the fastest alliums to cook. Sliced, they are ready in 5 to 10 minutes; whole or halved, give them 15 to 25 minutes depending on the method. The key is to wash them well and check for tenderness with a knife. Start with sautéed leeks this week: they are simple, quick, and a great way to use your garden harvest. If you grow your own, harvest them at 1 to 2 inches in diameter for the most tender texture.
