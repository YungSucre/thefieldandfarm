If you're looking for the best corn mazes in Minnesota, start with these top picks: Sever's Festival in Henderson, The Minnesota Corn Maze in Waconia, and Dehn's Pumpkins & More in Maple Plain. Each offers a unique experience, but all provide well-maintained mazes, family activities, and clear signage. Plan your visit between mid-September and late October, and expect to spend $10 to $20 per person for admission.

## Top Corn Mazes in Minnesota for 2026

### Sever's Festival (Henderson, MN)

Sever's is one of the largest and most popular corn mazes in the state. The maze covers 20 acres, with trails totaling over 5 miles. It's known for its themed designs, which change yearly. In 2026, expect a design celebrating Minnesota's 175th anniversary. Admission is $15 for adults, $12 for kids (ages 4-12), and free for under 4. Open weekends from mid-September through October, plus select weekdays.

### The Minnesota Corn Maze (Waconia, MN)

This family-owned farm offers a 12-acre maze with a 3-mile trail. It's less crowded than Sever's, making it ideal for younger children. Admission is $12 for adults, $10 for kids. They also have a petting zoo, corn cannon, and a giant jumping pillow. Open daily from late September to October 31.

### Dehn's Pumpkins & More (Maple Plain, MN)

Dehn's features a 10-acre maze with a shorter, more manageable trail (about 1.5 miles). It's perfect for families with toddlers. Admission is $10 per person, with a family pass for $35 (up to 5 people). They offer a pumpkin patch, hay bale maze, and a corn pit. Open weekends in October.

## When to Go: Timing and Crowds

- **Best time:** Weekday mornings (Tuesday-Thursday) are least crowded. Weekends are busiest, especially after 1 PM.
- **Peak season:** Late September to mid-October, when the corn is fully grown and the weather is mild.
- **Avoid:** Rainy days, as paths become muddy and slippery. Check the forecast before you go.

## What to Bring and Wear

- **Footwear:** Closed-toe shoes with good traction. Sneakers or hiking boots work best. Avoid sandals.
- **Clothing:** Layers. Mornings can be cool, but afternoons warm up. Long pants protect against corn leaves.
- **Extras:** Sunscreen, water bottle, and a phone with a fully charged battery. Some mazes have phone apps with maps, but don't rely on them.
- **Cost:** Budget $10-$20 per person for admission, plus extra for food, pumpkins, and activities. A family of four can easily spend $60-$100 for a full day.

## How to Navigate a Corn Maze Like a Pro

1. **Get a map** at the entrance. Most mazes provide a paper map or a QR code for a digital version.
2. **Use landmarks:** Look for tall towers or flags to orient yourself.
3. **Mark your path:** Use your phone to take photos at intersections, or drop a small piece of string (biodegradable) if allowed.
4. **Stay calm:** If you're lost, stop and listen for other visitors or staff. Most mazes have emergency exits.
5. **Time yourself:** On average, it takes 45 minutes to 2 hours to complete a maze, depending on size.

## Other Activities to Consider

Most corn mazes are part of larger fall festivals. Look for these add-ons:

- **Pumpkin patches:** Pick your own pumpkins, often $5-$10 each.
- **Hayrides:** Usually $5-$8 extra per person.
- **Corn cannons:** Shoot corn cobs at targets, $3-$5 per shot.
- **Food stands:** Apple cider, caramel apples, and roasted corn are common. Prices range $3-$8.

## Costs and Budgeting for 2026

Admission prices have held steady from 2025, but expect a slight increase due to rising fuel and labor costs. Here's a realistic breakdown:

- **Individual admission:** $10-$15
- **Family pass (4-5 people):** $35-$50
- **Parking:** Free at most locations, but some charge $5.
- **Total day out (family of 4):** $60-$120, including food and a pumpkin.

## FAQ

### Are corn mazes open in the rain?

Most open regardless, but paths get muddy. Check the farm's social media for closures. If it's raining, consider rescheduling for safety.

### Can I bring my dog?

Usually no, unless it's a service animal. Farms have livestock and food areas, so pets are typically not allowed.

### How long does it take to go through a corn maze?

It varies: small mazes take 20-30 minutes, large ones can take 2 hours. Plan for at least an hour at any of these top spots.

### Are there discounts for groups?

Yes, many farms offer group rates for 15 or more. Call ahead or check the website. Some also have military or senior discounts.

## The Bottom Line

The best corn maze in Minnesota depends on your priorities. For a big, challenging experience, go to Sever's Festival. For a quieter, family-friendly day, try The Minnesota Corn Maze or Dehn's. Visit on a weekday morning, wear sturdy shoes, and bring cash (some farms have ATMs but charge fees). With these tips, you'll have a memorable fall outing without the frustration of getting lost.

Happy maze navigating!
