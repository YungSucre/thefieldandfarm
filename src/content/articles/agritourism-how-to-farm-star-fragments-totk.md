Star Fragments in Tears of the Kingdom are rare materials used for armor upgrades and elixirs. To farm them efficiently, you need to know where they fall, when to look, and how to maximize your chances. This guide covers the best methods, from sky islands to surface hunting, with realistic expectations for drop rates and time investment.

## Understanding Star Fragment Drops

Star Fragments fall from the sky at night, typically between 9 PM and 3 AM in game time. They appear as glowing streaks and land somewhere on the surface or sky islands. Each night, a limited number fall, and they despawn by morning. Th

## Best Locations for Farming

### Sky Islands
Sky islands are prime spots because they offer high vantage points and fewer obstacles. The Great Sky Island and the areas around the Temple of Time are good. Stand on a high point and scan the horizon for falling stars. When you see one, mark it and glide to the landing spot.

### Surface Spots
On the surface, open fields like Hyrule Field or the Gerudo Highlands offer clear views. Avoid dense forests or mountains where the star might land in hard-to-reach places. A flat area with a wide sightline is ideal.

### The Dueling Peaks Tower
This tower is a classic spot. Climb to the top, wait for night, and watch for stars. The tower's height gives you a broad view of the surrounding area, and many stars land within gliding distance.

## Step-by-Step Farming Routine

1. **Set your in-game time to night**: Use a campfire or bed to skip to 9 PM. If you're in a hurry, wait until night at a stable.
2. **Travel to a high vantage point**: Use the Skyview Towers or climb a tall mountain. The Great Plateau Tower is a good starting point.
3. **Scan the horizon**: Look for a falling star. It appears as a bright streak with a trail. Once it lands, a beam of light marks the spot for a few seconds.
4. **Glide or ride to the landing site**: Use your paraglider or a horse if it's on the surface. Be quick, as the light fades and the star may be hard to find.
5. **Pick up the Star Fragment**: Walk up to it and press A. It will be added to your inventory.
6. **Repeat**: If you don't see one, wait until the next night by a campfire. You can also travel to a different region to reset the spawn.

## Maximizing Your Chances

- **Use the Sensor+**: If you've upgraded your Purah Pad, set the sensor to Star Fragments. It will beep when one is nearby, but it only works within a limited range.
- **Check the sky islands**: Stars often land on sky islands, and they're easier to spot from above. Use the Skyview Towers to launch into the air and scan.
- **Be patient**: On average, you might get 1-3 Star Fragments per in-game night if you're diligent. Some nights you'll get none.
- **Avoid rain**: Rain can obscure visibility. If it's raining, wait for clear weather.

## Realistic Yields and Time Investment

In a typical farming session of 1-2 real hours, you can expect to collect 2-5 Star Fragments, depending on luck and efficiency. With the Sensor+ and a good route, you might get more. Remember, they despawn at 5 AM, so don't sleep in.

## Tools and Equipment

- **Paraglider**: Essential for reaching landing spots.
- **Horse**: Useful for covering ground quickly on the surface.
- **Glider suit or stamina meals**: If you're gliding a lot, stamina recovery items help.
- **Sensor+**: Optional but helpful.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I farm Star Fragments during the day?**
A: No, they only fall at night. You must wait until 9 PM.

**Q: Do Star Fragments respawn?**
A: Yes, they respawn each night, but not in the same spot. The game generates new falls each night.

**Q: What's the best armor upgrade that uses Star Fragments?**
A: The Ancient Hero's Aspect and some armor sets like the Fierce Deity set require them. Check with the Great Fairy fountains for specific needs.

**Q: Can I use the Blood Moon to predict Star Fragments?**
A: No, Blood Moons don't affect Star Fragment spawns. They are independent.

## The Bottom Line

Farming Star Fragments in Tears of the Kingdom is a test of patience and observation. Use high vantage points, scan the sky at night, and be ready to move fast. With practice, you'll develop a rhythm that nets you a steady supply. Remember, it's a game of chance, so don't get discouraged if you come up empty some nights. Keep at it, and you'll have enough for all your upgrades.
