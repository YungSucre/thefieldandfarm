If you're looking for the best corn maze in Pennsylvania, start with Cherry Crest Adventure Farm in Lancaster County. It's consistently rated the top maze in the state, with a 5-acre design that changes yearly, plus 30+ attractions. For a more rustic experience, consider Klingel's Farm Market in Beaver County or Hellerick's Family Farm in Bucks County. ## Top Corn Mazes in Pennsylvania for 2026

Here are the standout mazes, based on reputation, visitor reviews, and amenities:

### 1. Cherry Crest Adventure Farm (Ronks, Lancaster County)

- **Maze size**: 5 acres, with a new design each year (2026 theme: "American Pride").
- **Features**: 2 miles of paths, 5 bridges, a 3-story observation tower, and 30+ attractions including a giant slide and corn cannon.
- **Ticket prices**: $22.95 for adults, $19.95 for children (ages 3-11), free for under 3. Group rates available.
- **Season**: Open daily from mid-September to November 2, 2026, 9 AM to 6 PM.
- **Why it's best**: The maze is meticulously maintained, and the farm offers a full day of activities. It's the most awarded maze in the country, winning multiple "Best Corn Maze" titles.

### 2. Klingel's Farm Market (Beaver Falls, Beaver County)

- **Maze size**: 3 acres, with a 2-mile path and a "haunted" version on Friday nights.
- **Features**: Pumpkin patch, hayrides, corn cannon, and a farm market with local produce.
- **Ticket prices**: $12 for adults, $10 for children (ages 4-12), free for under 4. Haunted maze: $15.
- **Season**: Open weekends only from September 20 to October 26, 2026, 10 AM to 5 PM.
- **Why it's a top pick**: It's affordable and family-friendly, with a more intimate feel. The haunted maze is a bonus for older kids.

### 3. Hellerick's Family Farm (Doylestown, Bucks County)

- **Maze size**: 5 acres, with a 3-mile path and a "maze challenge" that takes about 90 minutes to complete.
- **Features**: Hayrides, pumpkin patch, farm animals, and a corn pit for kids.
- **Ticket prices**: $15.95 for adults, $13.95 for children (ages 3-12), free for under 3.
- **Season**: Open daily from September 27 to November 1, 2026, 10 AM to 6 PM.
- **Why it's a top pick**: It's a well-run farm with a challenging maze and a great pumpkin selection. The farm's bakery is a bonus.

### 4. Shady Brook Farm (Yardley, Bucks County)

- **Maze size**: 4 acres, with a "haunted" maze on weekends.
- **Features**: Hayrides, corn cannon, farm animals, and a large pumpkin patch.
- **Ticket prices**: $14.95 for adults, $12.95 for children (ages 3-12), free for under 3. Haunted maze: $18.
- **Season**: Open daily from September 20 to November 2, 2026, 10 AM to 6 PM.
- **Why it's a top pick**: It's a popular spot with a lively atmosphere, and the haunted maze is well done.

## How to Choose the Right Maze for You

Consider your group's needs:

- **Families with young kids**: Choose a smaller maze like Klingel's or Hellerick's, which are less overwhelming. Look for mazes with a "kids zone" or short path.
- **Teens and adults**: Go for the challenge of Cherry Crest or Hellerick's, which have longer paths and more complex designs.
- **Budget**: Klingel's is the most affordable at $12, while Cherry Crest is the most expensive at $22.95. Check for online discounts or group rates.
- **Haunted mazes**: If you want a scare, visit Klingel's on a Friday night or Shady Brook on weekends. These are not suitable for young children.

## Practical Tips for Your Visit

- **Check the weather**: Corn mazes are muddy after rain. Wear boots or old shoes.
- **Go early**: Arrive at opening time to avoid crowds and have fresh paths.
- **Bring water**: You'll be walking for 1-2 hours. Some farms have water stations, but not all.
- **Use the map**: Most mazes provide a map or app. Don't rely on memory; it's easy to get turned around.
- **Take breaks**: Rest at the observation towers or benches. It's a workout.
- **Buy tickets online**: Many farms offer a $2 discount if you purchase in advance.

## What to Expect in 2026

Prices have risen about 5% from 2025, reflecting increased costs for seed, fuel, and labor. Expect to pay $12 to $25 per person. Most mazes are open from mid-September to early November, with peak weekends in October. Some farms offer evening hours with lights, which are magical but can be crowded.

## FAQ

### When is the best time to visit a corn maze?

The best time is late September to mid-October, when the corn is fully grown and the weather is mild. Avoid weekends in October if you prefer fewer crowds; go on a weekday or Sunday morning.

### Are corn mazes suitable for young children?

Yes, most mazes have a shorter path or a "kids zone." Cherry Crest and Hellerick's have specific areas for children. However, the full maze can be tiring for little legs, so plan for breaks.

### Can I bring my dog?

Most farms do not allow pets, except service animals. Check the farm's website before you go. Some farms have a "dog day" event, but it's rare.

### Do I need to book tickets in advance?

It's recommended, especially for weekends. Many farms cap daily attendance, and online tickets often come with a discount. Walk-ins are usually accepted, but you risk being turned away if it's sold out.

## The Bottom Line

For the best overall experience, choose Cherry Crest Adventure Farm. It's the most awarded, has the most attractions, and offers a full day of fun. If you're on a budget or want a quieter visit, Klingel's is a solid choice. For a challenging maze and great pumpkins, Hellerick's is excellent. Whichever you pick, go early, wear comfortable shoes, and enjoy the crisp fall air. Check each farm's website for 2026 dates and ticket prices, and book online to save a few dollars.
