The best way to hatch Pokemon eggs is to use the "Magma Armor/Flame Body" ability to halve hatch steps, then ride a fast Pokemon (like Koraidon or Miraidon) in a straight line in a loop, such as the path outside the nursery in Mesagoza. This method cuts hatching time by about 50% and is the fastest, most reliable approach in Pokemon Scarlet and Violet, and similar principles apply in other games.

## How Egg Hatching Works

In most Pokemon games, eggs hatch after you walk a certain number of steps. That number varies by species, but the game counts steps as you move. The key is to reduce the step count and maximize your movement speed.

- **Step count**: Each species has a base hatch cycle (e.g., 5,120 steps for most, 10,240 for others).
- **Abilities**: Pokemon with Flame Body (e.g., Magmar, Talonflame) or Magma Armor (e.g., Magcargo) cut the required steps in half. This is the single most important factor.
- **Movement**: Riding a fast Poke

## The Fastest Method in Pokemon Scarlet and Violet

In the current generation (Scarlet and Violet, 2022 and later), the best method is:

1. **Get a Pokemon with Flame Body or Magma Armor** at the front of your party. Talonflame is easy to catch early, or use Fletchinder. In Paldea, you can find Fletchinder near the starting area.
2. **Get a fast ride Pokemon**: Koraidon or Miraidon (your legendary) is the fastest. If you haven't unlocked its full speed, progress the story until you can ride it at high speed.
3. **Find a straight, flat route**: The best spot is the path just outside the nursery in Mesagoza (the city with the school). It is a long, straight road with no obstacles. Alternatively, use the long bridge near the Pokemon League.
4. **Hold the egg(s)**: Keep the eggs in your party. You can hold up to 5 eggs at once, plus the Flame Body Pokemon, so you hatch 5 at a time.
5. **Ride back and forth**: Hold the left stick forward and the ride button. You will automatically run in a straight line. When you hit the end, turn around and go back. Each loop takes about 30 seconds.

This method hatches most eggs in 2 to 4 minutes, depending on the species. For example, a standard 5,120-step egg becomes 2,560 steps with Flame Body, which is about 1 to 2 minutes of riding.

## Alternative Methods for Other Games

If you are playing older games, the principle is the same, but the tools differ:

- **Sword and Shield**: Use the bike on Route 5, which has a long straight path. The bike is faster than running. Same Flame Body trick.
- **Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl**: Use the bike on Route 209 or the Cycling Road. The bike is essential.
- **Legends: Arceus**: There is no breeding in this game, so no eggs to hatch.

## Tools and Items That Help

- **Oval Charm**: Obtained after completing the Pokedex, it increases the chance of eggs appearing at the nursery. Not strictly needed for hatching, but it speeds up the process of getting eggs.
- **Egg Power (Sandwiches)**: In Scarlet and Violet, eating a sandwich with Egg Power Level 2 or 3 (e.g., a Great Peanut Butter Sandwich) increases egg spawn rate at the nursery. This is about the picnic mechanic, not hatching, but it means you get eggs faster. A typical sandwich costs about 1,000 to 2,000 Pokedollars in ingredients.
- **Hatching Power (in older games)**: In X and Y, O-Power Hatching Power increases hatch speed. In Sun and Moon, you can use the Poke Pelago, but it is slower.

## Step-by-Step: Hatching in Scarlet and Violet

1. **Prepare your party**: Put your Flame Body Pokemon (e.g., Talonflame) in the first slot. Fill the rest with eggs (up to 5).
2. **Set a picnic**: At the nursery, set up a picnic to get eggs. Use a sandwich with Egg Power to get eggs faster. You can get 5 eggs in about 2 minutes.
3. **Collect eggs**: Talk to the basket in the picnic to collect eggs.
4. **Ride to the straight path**: Go to the road outside the nursery.
5. **Start riding**: Hold forward and the ride button. When you reach the end, turn around and ride back. Repeat until eggs hatch.
6. **Check for hatches**: When an egg hatches, the new Pokemon will appear. You can continue riding to hatch the rest.

This method is the most efficient because it combines the step reduction with the fastest movement speed in the game.

## Realistic Yield and Time

In a typical session, you can hatch 5 eggs in about 5 to 10 minutes, including the time to get eggs. If you are breeding for a shiny or specific IVs, you might hatch 100 eggs in an hour, which is a realistic rate with this method. Do not expect to hatch 100 eggs in 10 minutes; that is not possible.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Not using a Flame Body Pokemon**: This doubles your hatching time. Always have one in your party.
- **Running in circles**: Short loops with turns waste time because you slow down. Use a straight line.
- **Forgetting to collect eggs**: If you do not check the basket, you will not get new eggs.
- **Using a slow ride Pokemon**: Some players use a low-level ride Pokemon; always use Koraidon or Miraidon at full speed.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need a specific Pokemon for Flame Body?**
A: No, any Pokemon with Flame Body or Magma Armor works. Talonflame, Magmar, and Magcargo are common. In Scarlet and Violet, Fletchinder is easy to catch early.

**Q: Can I hatch eggs without riding?**
A: Yes, you can walk, but it is much slower. Riding is the fastest way.

**Q: Does the game count steps when I am in the menu or picnic?**
A: No, only when you are moving in the overworld. So avoid menus.

**Q: What is the fastest egg to hatch?**
A: Eggs with a low step count, like Magikarp (1,280 steps), hatch fastest. With Flame Body, that is 640 steps, which is about 30 seconds of riding.

## The Bottom Line

The best way to hatch Pokemon eggs is to combine a Flame Body Pokemon with the fastest ride Pokemon and a straight, flat route. In Scarlet and Violet, the path outside the nursery in Mesagoza is ideal. This method is simple, requires no special items beyond the Pokemon, and reduces hatching time by half. Whether you are breeding for a shiny or a competitive team, this is the approach to use.
