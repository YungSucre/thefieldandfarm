Stardust is the most valuable resource in Pokémon GO. You need it to power up Pokémon, unlock second moves, and trade. If you're short on Stardust, you're not alone. Here's how to farm it efficiently, using methods that work in 2026.

## Catch Everything, But Prioritize

The most direct way to earn Stardust is catching Pokémon. Each catch gives you 100 Stardust base, but you can boost that with multipliers. Use a Star Piece (costs 100 PokéCoins for 30 minutes) to get 1.5x Stardust. Also, catch Pokémon that give bonus Stardust: evolved forms (e.g., Pidgeot gives 300), and certain events like Spotlight Hours often have 2x or 3x catch Stardust.

**Pro tip:** Save your Star Pieces for events with boosted Stardust, like Community Day or special research days. You'll get more bang for your coin.

## Use Weather Boost and Curveballs

Weather-boosted Pokémon (shown with a weather icon) give 25% more Stardust. Always aim for curveballs and excellent throws; they don't add Stardust directly, but they increase catch rate, so you spend less time per catch. The key is volume: catch as many as you can in a short time.

## Battle in Go Battle League

Go Battle League (GBL) rewards Stardust at the end of each set (5 battles). You get Stardust even if you lose, but winning gives more. At rank 20 and above, you can earn up to 3,000 Stardust per set with a Star Piece. That's 15,000 Stardust per day if you do all 5 sets. Not bad for 30 minutes of battling.

**Realistic numbers:** In 2026, a typical GBL set (5 battles) gives 1,000-3,000 Stardust depending on rank and win streak. Use a Star Piece to multiply that by 1.5.

## Hatch Eggs Efficiently

Eggs give Stardust when they hatch. 2km eggs give 400-800, 5km eggs give 800-1,600, 10km eggs give 1,600-3,200, and 12km eggs give 2,400-4,800. To farm eggs, you need incubators. The infinite incubator is free, but you'll want to buy the Adventure Box (usually 1,480 PokéCoins) which includes 10-16 incubators. That's about $10-15 in real money, but you can earn PokéCoins by holding gyms (50 per day max).

**Tip:** Hatch 2km eggs in bulk during events with 2x hatch Stardust. You can hatch 9 at once if you have 9 incubators running.

## Use Research Tasks and Events

Field research tasks often reward Stardust. For example, "Catch 5 Pokémon" gives 500 Stardust. During special events, you can get tasks that give 1,000-2,000 Stardust. Also, the daily Timed Research often gives Stardust as a reward. Always check your research tab.

## Trade Pokémon for Stardust

Trading gives Stardust based on friendship level and distance. You get 100 Stardust per trade, but if you trade a Pokémon caught far away (over 100km), you get 500 Stardust. With a Star Piece, that's 750 per trade. You can do 100 trades per day, so that's up to 75,000 Stardust per day if you have the Pokémon and friends. That's a lot, but it requires preparation.

**How to get distance Pokémon:** Use the Pokémon GO Friend Code subreddit or Discord to find friends from other countries. When you trade, you'll get the distance bonus.

## Team Rocket Grunts and Leaders

Defeating Team Rocket Grunts gives you 500 Stardust, and Leaders give 1,000. You can battle them using Rocket Radars (earned from defeating 6 grunts). You can also get Stardust from purifying Shadow Pokémon, but that costs Stardust, so it's not a farming method.

## Daily Bonuses

Your first catch of the day gives 300 Stardust (500 with Star Piece). Your first spin of a PokéStop gives 100 Stardust. These are small, but they add up over a week.

## Use Star Pieces Wisely

Star Pieces cost 100 PokéCoins each, or you can get them in boxes. They last 30 minutes. To maximize, plan a 30-minute session where you do all of the following: catch Pokémon, battle in GBL, hatch eggs (if they're about to hatch), and trade. That way, you get the 1.5x multiplier on everything.

## Track Your Progress

Use the in-game journal to see how much Stardust you earn per day. Aim for 20,000-50,000 Stardust per day if you play for 1-2 hours. That's realistic with the methods above.

## FAQ

### How much Stardust can I earn per day?

With 1-2 hours of play, you can earn 20,000-50,000 Stardust per day using a Star Piece and focusing on catching, GBL, and trading. Hardcore players can exceed 100,000 on event days.

### Is it worth buying Star Pieces?

Yes, if you use them during active play. A Star Piece costs 100 coins (about $1), but it can double your Stardust for 30 minutes. If you earn 10,000 Stardust in that time, you get an extra 5,000, which is worth it.

### What's the best way to get Stardust fast for a new player?

Focus on catching everything, especially weather-boosted Pokémon. Use your free Star Pieces from level-up rewards. Also, join the GBL as soon as you reach level 10, even if you lose, you get Stardust.

### Do I need to spend real money to farm Stardust?

No, you can earn PokéCoins by defending gyms (50 per day) and use them for Star Pieces and incubators. It's slower, but possible. You can also get Star Pieces from special research and events.

## The bottom line

Farming Stardust in Pokémon GO is about efficiency: use Star Pieces during active play, catch as many as you can, battle in GBL, and trade with friends. Set a daily goal, track your progress, and you'll see your Stardust grow. It's not a get-rich-quick scheme, but with consistent effort, you'll have plenty for powering up your favorites.
