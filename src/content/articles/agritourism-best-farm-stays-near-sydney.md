## Top Farm Stays Near Sydney

### 1. Bilpin Farm Stay (Blue Mountains)

- **Distance from Sydney:** 90 minutes by car
- **Price:** $180-$250 per night (self-contained cottage)
- **Best for:** Families with kids who want to feed alpacas and collect eggs
- **What you get:** Two-bedroom cottage, private paddocks, and daily animal feeding sessions. The owners run a small orchard, so you can pick seasonal fruit (apples in autumn, berries in summer).
- **Booking tip:** Book 3-4 months ahead for school holidays. They often have a two-night minimum on weekends.

### 2. The Farm at Joadja (Southern Highlands)

- **Distance from Sydney:** 2 hours
- **Price:** $250-$350 per night (includes breakfast hamper)
- **Best for:** Couples or small groups wanting a luxury working farm experience
- **What you get:** Modern studio with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking rolling pastures. You can join the morning feed (cattle, sheep, and free-range pigs) or just watch from the deck. They also have a farm shop selling their own beef and lamb.
- **Booking tip:** Check their website for last-minute midweek deals (often 20% off).

### 3. Willow Farm Stay (Hunter Valley)

- **Distance from Sydney:** 2.5 hours (worth the extra drive)
- **Price:** $150-$220 per night (cottage with kitchen)
- **Best for:** Budget-conscious families who want a real farm stay without the luxury price tag
- **What you get:** A 100-acre working farm with horses, goats, and chickens. The cottage is basic but clean, with a full kitchen. You can help with milking (if you are there at 7am) or just wander the paddocks.
- **Booking tip:** They offer a 10% discount if you book directly on their website instead of Airbnb.

### 4. Wildwood Farm (Kangaroo Valley)

- **Distance from Sydney:** 2 hours
- **Price:** $200-$300 per night (glamping tent with shared bathroom)
- **Best for:** Adventurous types who want to sleep under canvas but still have a comfy bed
- **What you get:** Safari-style tents on a 50-acre property with a creek and resident kangaroos. Breakfast is included, and you can book a farm tour ($20 per person) to see the vegetable gardens and meet the cows.
- **Booking tip:** Bring your own towels and torches; the site is off-grid but has solar power.

## How to Choose the Right Farm Stay

- **Set your budget:** Prices range from $150 to $350 per night. Add $20-$50 per person for meals if not included.
- **Check the season:** Spring (September-November) is ideal for baby animals. Autumn (March-May) is best for harvest activities.
- **Read recent reviews:** Look for comments about cleanliness, host responsiveness, and whether the farm activities match the description.
- **Ask about farm work:** Some stays let you help with feeding, but others are just accommodation. Be clear on what you expect.

## Booking Tips for 2026

- **Book early for school holidays:** December and January fill up 6 months in advance.
- **Use direct booking sites:** Many farms offer 5-10% discounts if you book via their own website, avoiding Airbnb fees.
- **Check cancellation policies:** Most require 7-14 days notice for a full refund. Weather can change plans, so read the fine print.
- **Consider weekdays:** Prices drop by 20-30% from Sunday to Thursday.

## What to Pack for a Farm Stay

- **Sturdy boots** (gumboots are ideal for muddy paddocks)
- **Long pants and long sleeves** to protect against scratches and insects
- **Sunscreen and a hat** (farm sun is harsh)
- **Insect repellent** (especially in summer)
- **A torch** for night walks to the bathroom (if in a tent or cabin)
- **Your own food** if the stay is self-catering; check if there is a fridge and stove

## FAQ

### Are farm stays suitable for young children?

Yes, most farm stays welcome children, but always confirm safety measures. Ask about fencing, ponds, and animal temperaments. Some farms have age restrictions for certain activities, like tractor rides.

### Can I visit a farm stay without staying overnight?

Some farms offer day visits or tours, but most prefer overnight guests to manage farm operations. Check the farm's website for day tour options, which often cost $20-$50 per person.

### What is the best time of year for a farm stay near Sydney?

Spring (September-November) is best for newborn animals and mild weather. Autumn (March-May) offers harvest activities and cooler temperatures. Summer can be hot and busy, but school holidays are popular.

### Do farm stays provide meals?

It varies. Some include breakfast or a hamper, while others are self-catering with a kitchen. Always check what is included in the price before booking.

## The Bottom Line

The best farm stay near Sydney depends on your budget and what you want to do. For families, Bilpin Farm Stay offers the most hands-on experience at a fair price. For a romantic escape, The Farm at Joadja is worth the splurge. Book early, pack appropriately, and expect a real working farm, not a polished resort. You will leave with muddy boots and great memories.
