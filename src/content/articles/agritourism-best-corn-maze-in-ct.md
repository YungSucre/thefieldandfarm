If you are looking for the best corn maze in Connecticut, start with these top farms: Lyman Orchards in Middlefield, Fort Hill Farms in Thompson, and The Corn Maze at Winding Trails in Farmington. Each offers a unique experience, with maze sizes from 3 to 10 acres, and ticket prices ranging from $12 to $20 per person. This guide covers what makes each stand out, plus practical tips for visiting with kids or a group.

## Top Corn Mazes in Connecticut

### Lyman Orchards (Middlefield)
Lyman Orchards is a 1,200-acre farm known for its apple picking and a massive 10-acre corn maze. The maze is designed with a new theme each year, and it is split into two sections: one for families with younger kids (about 3 acres) and a more challenging section for older kids and adults. Expect to spend 45 to 90 minutes inside. Tickets are $14 for ages 13 and up, $10 for ages 4 to 12, and free for 3 and under. Open weekends from mid-September through October, 10 AM to 6 PM. They also have a corn maze flashlight night on select Fridays and Saturdays, which costs an extra $5.

### Fort Hill Farms (Thompson)
Fort Hill Farms is a working dairy farm that hosts a 6-acre corn maze with a farm-themed design. It is less crowded than Lyman, making it a good choice for families who want a quieter experience. The maze includes a scavenger hunt with 10 checkpoints, and you can rent a maze map for $1 (or use your phone to scan QR codes). Tickets are $12 for adults, $8 for children 4 to 12, and free for 3 and under. Open daily from late September to October 31, 10 AM to 5 PM. They also have a pumpkin patch and hayrides, which are included with admission.

### Winding Trails (Farmington)
Winding Trails is a recreation area that hosts a 5-acre corn maze, but it is not a farm; it is a nonprofit outdoor center. The maze is designed with a sports theme, and it is known for its educational signage about corn and agriculture. Tickets are $15 for adults, $10 for children 3 to 12, and free for under 3. ## How to Choose the Right Maze for You

Consider these factors when picking a maze:

- **Maze size**: Larger mazes (6 to 10 acres) can take 1 to 2 hours to complete. Smaller mazes (3 to 5 acres) are better for young children or if you are short on time.
- **Age of your group**: If you have toddlers, look for mazes with a separate kids' section, like Lyman Orchards. For teens and adults, a more complex maze with multiple paths is more fun.
- **Crowds**: Lyman Orchards is the busiest, especially on weekends. Fort Hill Farms and Winding Trails are less crowded, especially on weekday mornings.
- **Extra activities**: Many farms offer pumpkin picking, hayrides, and farm animals. If you want a full day out, choose a farm with these extras, like Lyman or Fort Hill.
- **Location**: If you are in eastern CT, Fort Hill is closest. In central CT, Lyman and Winding Trails are convenient. For western CT, consider checking local farms like The Corn Maze at Silverman's Farm in Easton (4 acres, $13 for adults).

## Practical Tips for Your Visit

- **Buy tickets online**: Most farms offer a small discount (about $2 off) if you buy tickets in advance on their website. This also saves you time in line.
- **Go early or late**: Weekday mornings are the least crowded. On weekends, arrive right at opening time to avoid the midday rush.
- **Wear comfortable shoes**: You will be walking on uneven ground, and it can be dusty or muddy. Sneakers or boots are best.
- **Bring water**: Even in October, it can be warm, and you will be walking for an hour or more. Many farms have water fountains, but it is safer to bring your own bottle.
- **Use the map or app**: Most mazes provide a map or a phone app with GPS. Use it if you get lost; it is not cheating, it is practical.
- **Check the weather**: If it has rained recently, the maze can be muddy. Some farms close the maze if it is too wet, so call ahead or check their social media before you go.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are corn mazes suitable for young children?
Yes, but choose a maze with a designated kids' section, like Lyman Orchards. These sections have wider paths and are less confusing. Always stay with your child, and set a meeting point in case you get separated.

### Can I bring my dog?
Most corn mazes do not allow dogs, except for service animals. Check the farm's policy before you go. Fort Hill Farms allows leashed dogs on weekdays only.

### What happens if it rains?
Mazes are typically closed during heavy rain because the paths become muddy and unsafe. If it is just a light drizzle, they may stay open. Check the farm's website or call ahead. Many farms will refund or reschedule tickets if they close due to weather.

### How long does it take to complete a corn maze?
It depends on the size and your group's pace. A 5-acre maze can take 30 to 60 minutes, while a 10-acre maze can take 1 to 2 hours. If you are in a hurry, you can always exit early; there are emergency exits at intervals.

## The Bottom Line

The best corn maze in Connecticut for most people is Lyman Orchards because of its size, variety, and extra activities. But if you want a quieter, more relaxed experience, Fort Hill Farms is a great choice, and Winding Trails is ideal for a shorter visit. Whichever you pick, go on a weekday if possible, buy tickets online, and wear sturdy shoes. You will have a memorable fall day without the stress.
