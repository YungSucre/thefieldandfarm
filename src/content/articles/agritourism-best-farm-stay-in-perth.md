If you are looking for the best farm stay near Perth, start with these three: Fairbridge Farm Stay in Pinjarra, the Organic Farm Stay in Swan Valley, and the Sheep’s Back Farm in York. All are within 90 minutes of Perth, offer real farm experiences, and have solid reputations for 2026. Book early, especially for school holidays, and expect to pay between $180 and $350 per night depending on the season and accommodation type.

## What makes a farm stay great

A great farm stay is not just a bed in a barn. It is a chance to feed animals, collect eggs, and see how a working farm runs. The best ones have hosts who are happy to show you around, but also give you space. They are clean, safe, and honest about what is available. Avoid places that look 
![Best farm stay in perth](/images/articles/best-farm-stay-in-perth/section-2.jpg)
like hotels with a few farm props. You want real work, real animals, and real food.

## Top farm stays near Perth

### 1. Fairbridge Farm Stay, Pinjarra

Fairbridge is a classic. It sits on 73 hectares of bush and farmland, about 80 minutes south of Perth. You can stay in heritage cottages or bunkhouse style rooms. The farm has sheep, goats, chickens, and sometimes cattle. Kids can help with feeding and collecting eggs. There is also a playground and a pool.

- **Price**: $220 to $320 per night for a cottage, sleeps 4 to 6.
- **Best for**: families with young children.
- **Booking**: Book 2 to 3 months ahead for weekends.

### 2. The Organic Farm Stay, Swan Valley

This is a smaller, more intimate place in the Swan Valley wine region, about 30 minutes from Perth. The farm grows organic vegetables and keeps free-range chickens and a couple of pigs. You stay in a self-contained cottage with a kitchen. The host offers a daily farm tour at 4 pm, where you can feed the animals and pick seasonal produce.

- **Price**: $250 to $350 per night, includes breakfast hamper.
- **Best for**: couples or small families who want a quiet, food-focused stay.
- **Booking**: Book 4 to 6 weeks ahead, especially for spring and autumn.

### 3. Sheep’s Back Farm, York

York is about 90 minutes east of Perth, in the Avon Valley. This working sheep farm offers a renovated shearers’ quarters with modern amenities. You can help with sheep mustering if you visit during shearing season (August to September). Otherwise, you can walk the property, see the lambs, and try your hand at wool spinning.

- **Price**: $180 to $260 per night, sleeps 2 to 5.
- **Best for**: those who want a real working farm experience.
- **Booking**: Book 1 to 2 months ahead for weekends.

## How to choose the right one for you

Think about what you want from the stay. If you have kids under 10, Fairbridge is the safest bet because it has lots of activities and a pool. If you want a romantic weekend with good food, the Organic Farm Stay in Swan Valley is better. If you want to get your hands dirty and learn about sheep, Sheep’s Back Farm is the pick.

Also consider the season. Spring (September to November) is the best time for lambs and wildflowers. Autumn (March to May) is good for harvest activities and mild weather. Summer can be hot, so check if the accommodation has air conditioning. Winter is quiet and cheaper, but some farms reduce activities.

## What to expect during your stay

Most farm stays include a basic orientation on arrival. You will be shown the animals, the feeding schedule, and any safety rules. You are not expected to do heavy work unless you ask. But you can usually help with:

- Feeding chickens and collecting eggs
- Bottle-feeding lambs (if available)
- Checking water troughs
- Picking vegetables or fruit
- Mucking out stalls (if you are keen)

Wear closed-toe shoes, long pants, and a hat. Bring sunscreen and insect repellent. Farms are dusty and uneven, so leave your fancy clothes at home.

## Booking tips for 2026

- Book early for school holidays (April, July, September, and December). These fill up 3 to 6 months ahead.
- Use direct booking on the farm’s website when possible. You often get a better rate than through third-party sites.
- Ask about minimum stays. Some farms require 2 nights on weekends.
- Check cancellation policies. Many require 7 days notice for a full refund.
- If you are flexible, ask about last-minute deals. Some farms offer 20% off for bookings within 7 days.

## How to get there and what to bring

All three farms are accessible by car. Fairbridge is off the South Western Highway, about 80 minutes from Perth CBD. The Organic Farm Stay is off West Swan Road, about 30 minutes. Sheep’s Back Farm is off the Great Eastern Highway, about 90 minutes. You will need a car; public transport is not practical.

Bring:

- Sturdy shoes and old clothes
- A hat and sunscreen
- A water bottle
- Your own food if you have dietary restrictions (some farm stays provide meals, but not all)
- A camera for the kids

## FAQ

### Are farm stays suitable for toddlers?

Yes, but check with the farm first. Fairbridge is very toddler-friendly with a fenced playground and gentle animals. Sheep’s Back Farm has uneven ground, so it is better for older kids. Always supervise toddlers around animals.

### Do farm stays provide meals?

It varies. Fairbridge has a communal kitchen and you can cook your own meals. The Organic Farm Stay includes a breakfast hamper. Sheep’s Back Farm has a kitchenette, but you bring your own food. Check the listing before booking.

### Can I bring my dog?

Most farm stays do not allow pets because they can disturb livestock. Fairbridge does not allow dogs. The Organic Farm Stay might allow a small, well-behaved dog if you ask, but it is not guaranteed. Sheep’s Back Farm has working dogs, so no outside dogs.

### What happens if it rains?

Farm activities may be limited, but you can still enjoy the animals from under cover. Fairbridge has indoor play areas. The Organic Farm Stay has a veranda. Sheep’s Back Farm has a large common room. Always pack a raincoat just in case.

## The bottom line

The best farm stay near Perth depends on your group and what you want. For families, Fairbridge is the top pick. For couples, the Organic Farm Stay is hard to beat. For a real working farm experience, Sheep’s Back Farm delivers. Book early, pack for the outdoors, and go with an open mind. You will come home with dirty boots and great memories.
